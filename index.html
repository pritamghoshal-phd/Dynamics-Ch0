Dynamics, Interactively

Interactive notes for undergraduate dynamics, organized chapter by chapter. Each chapter explains the key ideas with animations you can play with, then works through every example in the book step by step, with free-body or kinematics diagrams, an animation of that exact problem and a 3D view of the same motion.

Every slider has a box next to it. Type any number into the box and press Enter to try your own values.

Chapters

Chapter 0

Mathematical Tools

Vectors, free-body diagrams, and the calculus of motion along a line.

 Chapter 1

Particle Kinematics

Describing how a point moves in the plane and in space.

Planar Rigid Body Kinematics

How the velocities and accelerations of points on a moving rigid body are related.

Moving Reference Frame Kinematics

Describing motion from a frame that itself translates and rotates, with real-life Coriolis animations.

 Chapter 4

Particle Kinetics

 Chapter 5

 Chapter 6

Vibrations

Equations of motion, free and damped vibration, and forced response, with challenges and badges.

Chapter structure and example problems follow Dynamics: A Lecturebook by Charles M. Krousgrill and Jeffrey F. Rhoads (Purdue University).
