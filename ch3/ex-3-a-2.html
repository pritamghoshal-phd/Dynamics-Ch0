Example 3.A.2Particle in a curved slot of a rolling disk

rolling diskcurved slotCoriolis

Problem

A disk of radius $R$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal surface. The speed and acceleration of its center O are $v_O$ and $a_O$, both to the right. A particle P slides in a curved slot cut in the disk. The slot is a circular arc of radius $R$. P moves along the slot with speed $u$ relative to the disk, and this relative speed changes at the rate $\dot u$.

The data are $v_O = 3$ m/s, $a_O = 5$ m/s², $u = 2$ m/s, $\dot u = 7$ m/s² and $R = 0.75$ m.

Find (a) the velocity of P when P is on the rim of the disk, directly to the left of O, and (b) the acceleration of P at the same position.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame and describe its motion

Attach the $xyz$ frame to the disk with its origin at O. The disk rolls without slipping to the right, so it turns clockwise, and $v_O = R\omega$ and $a_O = R\alpha$.

$$\vec\omega = -\frac{v_O}{R}\,\hat k = -4\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \vec\alpha = -\frac{a_O}{R}\,\hat k = -6.667\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}^2$$

The center moves on a straight line, so $\vec v_O = 3\,\hat\imath$ m/s and $\vec a_O = 5\,\hat\imath$ m/s².

Step 2 · Describe the motion of P relative to the frame

Seen from the disk, P moves on a circle of radius $R$ about the slot's center C, which is fixed in the disk at $(-R,\ R)$. At the position in question, P is at $\vec r_{P/O} = -R\,\hat\imath = -0.75\,\hat\imath$ m, directly below C. The slot is horizontal there and P moves to the right, so

$$\vec v_{rel} = u\,\hat\imath = 2\,\hat\imath\ \text{m/s}$$

The relative path is curved, so the relative acceleration has a tangential part $\dot u$ and a normal part $u^2/R$ that points toward the center C of the slot, which is straight up.

$$\vec a_{rel} = \dot u\,\hat\imath + \frac{u^2}{R}\,\hat\jmath = 7\,\hat\imath + 5.333\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}^2$$
$$\vec v_P = \vec v_O + \vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec v_{rel}$$
$$\vec a_P = \vec a_O + \vec\alpha\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec\omega\times\left(\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O}\right) + 2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} + \vec a_{rel}$$
Step 3 · Find the velocity of P
$$\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} = (-4\,\hat k)\times(-0.75\,\hat\imath) = 3\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$
$$\vec v_P = 3\,\hat\imath + 3\,\hat\jmath + 2\,\hat\imath = 5\,\hat\imath + 3\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$

A useful check is that P is on the rim. If P were fixed to the disk there, its velocity would be $3\,\hat\imath + 3\,\hat\jmath$ m/s, which is perpendicular to the line from the contact point to P. The relative velocity adds $2\,\hat\imath$ to this.

Step 4 · Find the acceleration of P
$$\vec\alpha\times\vec r_{P/O} = (-6.667\,\hat k)\times(-0.75\,\hat\imath) = 5\,\hat\jmath, \qquad -\omega^2\,\vec r_{P/O} = -16\,(-0.75\,\hat\imath) = 12\,\hat\imath$$
$$2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} = 2\,(-4\,\hat k)\times(2\,\hat\imath) = -16\,\hat\jmath$$

Adding $\vec a_O = 5\,\hat\imath$ and $\vec a_{rel} = 7\,\hat\imath + 5.333\,\hat\jmath$ gives, in m/s²,

$$\vec a_P = (5 + 12 + 7)\,\hat\imath + (5 - 16 + 5.333)\,\hat\jmath = 24\,\hat\imath - 5.67\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}^2$$
Answer

(a) $\vec v_P = 5\,\hat\imath + 3\,\hat\jmath$ m/s, with magnitude 5.83 m/s. (b) $\vec a_P = 24\,\hat\imath - 5.67\,\hat\jmath$ m/s², with magnitude 24.7 m/s².
