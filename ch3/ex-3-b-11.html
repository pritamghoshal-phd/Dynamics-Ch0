Example 3.B.11Disk on a spinning arm on a rotating shaft

3D framesangular accelerationthree rotations

Problem

A vertical shaft turns in fixed bearings at the constant rate $\omega_2$ about the $Y$ axis. The horizontal arm OB, of length $b$, is held by a collar at O so that it can spin about its own axis, and it spins at the constant rate $\omega_1$ relative to the collar. A disk of radius $r$ is mounted at B on an axle perpendicular to the disk and spins relative to the arm at the constant rate $\omega_3$. The $XYZ$ axes are fixed, and the $xyz$ axes are attached to the disk. At the instant shown, A is directly above the center B of the disk and the $xyz$ and $XYZ$ axes are aligned.

Find (a) the velocity and (b) the acceleration of the point A.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the reference frames

Let the $XYZ$ axes be fixed with $Y$ along the shaft. The shaft and collar turn at $\omega_2\hat J$. The arm turns with the collar and also spins about its own axis $\hat\imath$, so its angular velocity is $\omega_2\hat J + \omega_1\hat\imath$. The disk turns with the arm and spins about its axle $\hat k$. At this instant $\hat\imath = \hat I$, $\hat\jmath = \hat J$ and $\hat k = \hat K$. Point B is on the arm axis, so we use it as the base point for the disk.

Drawn with the sample values used in the animation (b = 0.5 m, r = 0.2 m, ω₁ = 1 rad/s, ω₂ = 1.2 rad/s, ω₃ = 3 rad/s).

Step 2 · Find the angular velocity of the disk
$$\vec\omega = \omega_2\hat J + \omega_1\hat\imath + \omega_3\hat k$$
Step 3 · Find the angular acceleration of the disk

All three rates are constant, so only the changing directions contribute. $\hat J$ is fixed. The arm axis $\hat\imath$ turns with the collar, and the axle $\hat k$ turns with the arm.

$$\frac{d\hat\imath}{dt} = \omega_2\hat J\times\hat\imath = -\omega_2\hat k, \qquad \frac{d\hat k}{dt} = \left(\omega_2\hat J + \omega_1\hat\imath\right)\times\hat k = \omega_2\hat\imath - \omega_1\hat\jmath$$
$$\vec\alpha = \omega_1\frac{d\hat\imath}{dt} + \omega_3\frac{d\hat k}{dt} = \omega_2\omega_3\,\hat\imath - \omega_1\omega_3\,\hat\jmath - \omega_1\omega_2\,\hat k$$
Step 4 · Find the motion of the center B

The spin of the arm about its own axis does not move B, so B moves on a horizontal circle of radius $b$.

$$\vec v_B = \omega_2\hat J\times b\,\hat\imath = -\omega_2 b\,\hat k, \qquad \vec a_B = -\omega_2^2 b\,\hat\imath$$
Step 5 · Find the velocity of A

With $\vec r_{A/B} = r\,\hat\jmath$,

$$\vec v_A = \vec v_B + \vec\omega\times\vec r_{A/B} = -\omega_2 b\,\hat k + \left(\omega_1\hat\imath + \omega_2\hat\jmath + \omega_3\hat k\right)\times r\,\hat\jmath = -\omega_3 r\,\hat\imath + \left(\omega_1 r - \omega_2 b\right)\hat k$$
Step 6 · Find the acceleration of A
$$\vec a_A = \vec a_B + \vec\alpha\times\vec r_{A/B} + \vec\omega\times\left(\vec\omega\times\vec r_{A/B}\right)$$
$$\vec\alpha\times r\,\hat\jmath = \omega_1\omega_2 r\,\hat\imath + \omega_2\omega_3 r\,\hat k, \qquad \vec\omega\times\left(\vec\omega\times r\,\hat\jmath\right) = \omega_1\omega_2 r\,\hat\imath - \left(\omega_1^2 + \omega_3^2\right) r\,\hat\jmath + \omega_2\omega_3 r\,\hat k$$
$$\vec a_A = \left(2\,\omega_1\omega_2 r - \omega_2^2 b\right)\hat\imath - \left(\omega_1^2 + \omega_3^2\right) r\,\hat\jmath + 2\,\omega_2\omega_3 r\,\hat k$$

With $\omega_1 = 0$ this reduces to the answer of Example 3.B.8, with the shaft rate and the disk spin renamed.

Answer

(a) The velocity of A is

$$\vec v_A = -\omega_3 r\,\hat\imath + \left(\omega_1 r - \omega_2 b\right)\hat k$$

(b) The acceleration of A is

$$\vec a_A = \left(2\,\omega_1\omega_2 r - \omega_2^2 b\right)\hat\imath - \left(\omega_1^2 + \omega_3^2\right) r\,\hat\jmath + 2\,\omega_2\omega_3 r\,\hat k$$

With the values of the animation ($b = 0.5$ m, $r = 0.2$ m, $\omega_1 = 1$, $\omega_2 = 1.2$, $\omega_3 = 3$ rad/s), $\vec v_A = -0.6\,\hat\imath - 0.4\,\hat k$ m/s and $\vec a_A = -0.24\,\hat\imath - 2.0\,\hat\jmath + 1.44\,\hat k$ m/s².
