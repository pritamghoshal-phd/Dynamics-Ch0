Example 3.A.3Fire-truck ladder raised and extended

translating baseCoriolispolar

Problem

A fire truck moves forward at a constant speed of 50 ft/s. Its ladder is pivoted at O on the truck. The ladder is raised at the constant rate $\dot\theta = 0.3$ rad/s and extended at the constant rate $\dot b = 2$ ft/s, where $b$ is the distance from O to the end P of the ladder.

Find the acceleration of the end P of the ladder when $b = 25$ ft and $\theta = 30°$.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame and describe its motion

Attach the $xyz$ frame to the ladder, with its origin at the pivot O and its $x$ axis along the ladder. The origin O moves with the truck at a constant velocity, and the frame turns with the ladder at a constant rate.

$$\vec a_O = \vec 0, \qquad \vec\omega = \dot\theta\,\hat k = 0.3\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \vec\alpha = \vec 0$$
Step 2 · Describe the motion of P relative to the frame

An observer on the ladder sees P move straight out along the ladder at a constant rate.

$$\vec r_{P/O} = b\,\hat\imath = 25\,\hat\imath\ \text{ft}, \qquad \vec v_{rel} = \dot b\,\hat\imath = 2\,\hat\imath\ \text{ft/s}, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = \ddot b\,\hat\imath = \vec 0$$
$$\vec v_P = \vec v_O + \vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec v_{rel}$$
$$\vec a_P = \vec a_O + \vec\alpha\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec\omega\times\left(\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O}\right) + 2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} + \vec a_{rel}$$
Step 3 · Find the acceleration in the ladder frame

Only two terms are not zero.

$$-\omega^2\,\vec r_{P/O} = -(0.3)^2(25)\,\hat\imath = -2.25\,\hat\imath\ \text{ft/s}^2, \qquad 2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} = 2\,(0.3\,\hat k)\times(2\,\hat\imath) = 1.2\,\hat\jmath\ \text{ft/s}^2$$
$$\vec a_P = -2.25\,\hat\imath + 1.2\,\hat\jmath\ \text{ft/s}^2, \qquad \left|\vec a_P\right| = 2.55\ \text{ft/s}^2$$

These are the polar components $\ddot b - b\dot\theta^2$ and $b\ddot\theta + 2\dot b\dot\theta$ from Chapter 1. The constant velocity of the truck adds nothing to the acceleration.

Step 4 · Write the result in fixed horizontal and vertical components

With $\theta = 30°$, the unit vectors of the ladder frame are $\hat\imath = \cos 30°\,\hat I + \sin 30°\,\hat J$ and $\hat\jmath = -\sin 30°\,\hat I + \cos 30°\,\hat J$. Therefore

$$\vec a_P = -2.25\,(0.866\,\hat I + 0.5\,\hat J) + 1.2\,(-0.5\,\hat I + 0.866\,\hat J) = -2.55\,\hat I - 0.086\,\hat J\ \text{ft/s}^2$$

The centripetal term points from P back toward O. The Coriolis term is perpendicular to the ladder, in the direction of rotation.

Answer

$\vec a_P = -2.25\,\hat\imath + 1.2\,\hat\jmath$ ft/s² in the ladder frame, which is $-2.55\,\hat I - 0.086\,\hat J$ ft/s² in fixed components. The magnitude is 2.55 ft/s².
