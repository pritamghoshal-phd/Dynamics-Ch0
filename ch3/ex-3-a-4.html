Example 3.A.4Pin in a slot of a spinning disk, held by a hanging link

slottwo descriptionsCoriolis

Problem

A disk turns counterclockwise about a fixed pin at O at the constant rate $\omega$. Pin P slides in a straight slot cut in the disk, and P is also the lower end of link AP, which hangs from a fixed pin at A. The $xyz$ axes are attached to the disk. The slot makes the angle $\theta$ with the $x$ axis. At the instant shown, P is on the $x$ axis, the $x$ axis is horizontal, and link AP is vertical.

The data are $\omega = 8$ rad/s, $r = 0.2$ m (the distance OP), $L = 0.3$ m (the length of AP) and $\theta = 36.87°$.

Find (a) the angular velocity of link AP and (b) the angular acceleration of link AP at this instant.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame

Attach the $xyz$ frame to the disk with its origin at the fixed pin O. Then

$$\vec v_O = \vec a_O = \vec 0, \qquad \vec\omega = 8\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \vec\alpha = \vec 0$$
Step 2 · Describe the motion of P relative to the disk

P is at $\vec r_{P/O} = r\,\hat\imath = 0.2\,\hat\imath$ m. Relative to the disk, P can only slide along the straight slot, whose direction is $\hat e = \cos\theta\,\hat\imath + \sin\theta\,\hat\jmath = 0.8\,\hat\imath + 0.6\,\hat\jmath$. Its relative speed $u$ and relative rate of change of speed $\dot u$ are unknown.

$$\vec v_{rel} = u\,\hat e, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = \dot u\,\hat e$$

The slot is straight, so the relative acceleration has no normal part.

Step 3 · Describe P a second way, as a point of link AP

P is also the end of link AP, which turns about the fixed pin A. With $\vec r_{P/A} = -L\,\hat\jmath$,

$$\vec v_P = \omega_{AP}\hat k\times(-L\,\hat\jmath) = L\,\omega_{AP}\,\hat\imath, \qquad \vec a_P = \alpha_{AP}\hat k\times(-L\,\hat\jmath) - \omega_{AP}^2\,(-L\,\hat\jmath) = L\,\alpha_{AP}\,\hat\imath + L\,\omega_{AP}^2\,\hat\jmath$$

The two descriptions must give the same velocity and the same acceleration for P. This gives two scalar equations each time.

Step 4 · Solve the velocity equations

With the moving frame,

$$\vec v_P = \vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec v_{rel} = \omega r\,\hat\jmath + u\,(0.8\,\hat\imath + 0.6\,\hat\jmath)$$

Setting this equal to $L\,\omega_{AP}\,\hat\imath$ and comparing components,

$$\hat\jmath:\ \ \omega r + 0.6\,u = 0 \ \Rightarrow\ u = -\frac{(8)(0.2)}{0.6} = -2.667\ \text{m/s}$$
$$\hat\imath:\ \ 0.8\,u = L\,\omega_{AP} \ \Rightarrow\ \omega_{AP} = \frac{0.8(-2.667)}{0.3} = -7.11\ \text{rad/s}$$

The negative value of $u$ means that P slides down and to the left along the slot.

Step 5 · Solve the acceleration equations

With the moving frame, the nonzero terms are

$$-\omega^2\,\vec r_{P/O} = -12.8\,\hat\imath, \qquad 2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} = 2(8)(-2.667)\,\hat k\times(0.8\,\hat\imath + 0.6\,\hat\jmath) = 25.6\,\hat\imath - 34.13\,\hat\jmath, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = \dot u\,(0.8\,\hat\imath + 0.6\,\hat\jmath)$$

Setting the sum equal to $L\,\alpha_{AP}\,\hat\imath + L\,\omega_{AP}^2\,\hat\jmath$, where $L\,\omega_{AP}^2 = 0.3(7.111)^2 = 15.17$ m/s²,

$$\hat\jmath:\ \ -34.13 + 0.6\,\dot u = 15.17 \ \Rightarrow\ \dot u = 82.17\ \text{m/s}^2$$
$$\hat\imath:\ \ -12.8 + 25.6 + 0.8\,(82.17) = 0.3\,\alpha_{AP} \ \Rightarrow\ \alpha_{AP} = 261.8\ \text{rad/s}^2$$
Answer

(a) $\vec\omega_{AP} = -7.11\,\hat k$ rad/s, which is 7.11 rad/s clockwise. (b) $\vec\alpha_{AP} = 262\,\hat k$ rad/s², which is 262 rad/s² counterclockwise.
