Example 3.A.1Particle sliding in a slot of a rolling disk

rolling diskCoriolismoving frame

Problem

A disk of radius $r$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal surface. At the instant shown, its center O moves to the right at $v_O = 5$ m/s, and this speed is decreasing at 2 m/s². A particle P slides in a straight slot of the disk. The slot is parallel to the disk's $x$ axis, at a height $h$ above O. At this instant P is at $x_P = 0.2$ m and moves along the slot at the constant rate $\dot x_P = 2$ m/s. Here $x_P$ is measured in the $xyz$ frame attached to the disk, whose axes are horizontal and vertical at this instant.

The data are $h = 0.2$ m and $r = 0.6$ m.

Find (a) the velocity of P and (b) the acceleration of P.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame and describe its motion

Attach the $xyz$ frame to the disk with its origin at the center O. The frame then turns with the disk, so its angular velocity is the angular velocity of the disk.

The disk rolls without slipping, so $v_O = r\omega$. It rolls to the right, which means it turns clockwise. Differentiating the rolling condition gives $\dot v_O = r\alpha$. Because the disk is slowing down, its angular acceleration is counterclockwise.

$$\vec\omega = -\frac{v_O}{r}\,\hat k = -\frac{5}{0.6}\,\hat k = -8.333\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \vec\alpha = -\frac{\dot v_O}{r}\,\hat k = -\frac{-2}{0.6}\,\hat k = 3.333\,\hat k\ \text{rad/s}^2$$

The center O moves on a straight line, so $\vec v_O = 5\,\hat\imath$ m/s and $\vec a_O = -2\,\hat\imath$ m/s².

Step 2 · Describe the motion of P relative to the frame

An observer riding on the disk sees P move along the straight slot at a constant rate. In the moving frame,

$$\vec r_{P/O} = x_P\,\hat\imath + h\,\hat\jmath = 0.2\,\hat\imath + 0.2\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m}, \qquad \vec v_{rel} = \dot x_P\,\hat\imath = 2\,\hat\imath\ \text{m/s}, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = \ddot x_P\,\hat\imath = \vec 0$$

At this instant the moving axes are horizontal and vertical, so the frame components are also the fixed components.

The moving-frame equations are

$$\vec v_P = \vec v_O + \vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec v_{rel}$$
$$\vec a_P = \vec a_O + \vec\alpha\times\vec r_{P/O} + \vec\omega\times\left(\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O}\right) + 2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} + \vec a_{rel}$$
Step 3 · Find the velocity of P

The term from the rotation of the frame is

$$\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O} = (-8.333\,\hat k)\times(0.2\,\hat\imath + 0.2\,\hat\jmath) = 1.667\,\hat\imath - 1.667\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$

Adding the three terms gives

$$\vec v_P = 5\,\hat\imath + (1.667\,\hat\imath - 1.667\,\hat\jmath) + 2\,\hat\imath = 8.67\,\hat\imath - 1.67\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$
Step 4 · Find the acceleration of P

Each term of the acceleration equation is computed separately.

$$\vec a_O = -2\,\hat\imath, \qquad \vec\alpha\times\vec r_{P/O} = (3.333\,\hat k)\times(0.2\,\hat\imath + 0.2\,\hat\jmath) = -0.667\,\hat\imath + 0.667\,\hat\jmath$$
$$\vec\omega\times(\vec\omega\times\vec r_{P/O}) = -\omega^2\,\vec r_{P/O} = -69.44\,(0.2\,\hat\imath + 0.2\,\hat\jmath) = -13.89\,\hat\imath - 13.89\,\hat\jmath$$
$$2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} = 2\,(-8.333\,\hat k)\times(2\,\hat\imath) = -33.33\,\hat\jmath, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = \vec 0$$

All values are in m/s². Their sum is

$$\vec a_P = -16.56\,\hat\imath - 46.56\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}^2$$

The Coriolis term is the largest one. It comes from P moving along the slot while the slot turns.

Answer

(a) $\vec v_P = 8.67\,\hat\imath - 1.67\,\hat\jmath$ m/s, with magnitude 8.83 m/s. (b) $\vec a_P = -16.6\,\hat\imath - 46.6\,\hat\jmath$ m/s², with magnitude 49.4 m/s².
