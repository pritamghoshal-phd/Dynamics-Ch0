Example 3.A.5Link OA driven by a pin in a rotating slot

mechanismslotvelocity

Problem

Links OA and BC are pinned to the ground at O and C, which are on the same horizontal line. Pin A at the end of link OA slides in a slot cut in link BC. Link OA is 500 mm long. The $xyz$ axes are attached to link BC. At the instant shown, link BC is vertical with $L = 400$ mm, where $L$ is the distance from C to A, and BC turns counterclockwise at $\omega = 6$ rad/s.

Find (a) the angular velocity of link OA and (b) the rate $\dot L$ at this instant.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame

Attach the $xyz$ frame to link BC with its origin at the fixed pin C. At this instant the $y$ axis points up along BC. Then $\vec v_C = \vec 0$ and $\vec\omega = 6\,\hat k$ rad/s.

Step 2 · Describe the motion of A relative to BC

Relative to link BC, pin A can only slide along the slot. So

$$\vec r_{A/C} = L\,\hat\jmath = 0.4\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m}, \qquad \vec v_{rel} = \dot L\,\hat\jmath$$
Step 3 · Describe A a second way, as a point of link OA

The distance OC follows from the right triangle OCA, $OC = \sqrt{0.5^2 - 0.4^2} = 0.3$ m. So $\vec r_{A/O} = 0.3\,\hat\imath + 0.4\,\hat\jmath$ m, and because OA turns about the fixed pin O,

$$\vec v_A = \omega_{OA}\hat k\times(0.3\,\hat\imath + 0.4\,\hat\jmath) = -0.4\,\omega_{OA}\,\hat\imath + 0.3\,\omega_{OA}\,\hat\jmath$$
Step 4 · Equate the two descriptions and solve

With the moving frame,

$$\vec v_A = \vec v_C + \vec\omega\times\vec r_{A/C} + \vec v_{rel} = 6\,\hat k\times 0.4\,\hat\jmath + \dot L\,\hat\jmath = -2.4\,\hat\imath + \dot L\,\hat\jmath$$

Comparing components with the expression from link OA,

$$\hat\imath:\ \ -0.4\,\omega_{OA} = -2.4 \ \Rightarrow\ \omega_{OA} = 6\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \hat\jmath:\ \ \dot L = 0.3\,\omega_{OA} = 1.8\ \text{m/s}$$

The velocity of A from link OA is perpendicular to OA. It is the sum of the rotation term and the sliding term.

The same method with the acceleration equation gives $\alpha_{OA} = 27$ rad/s² counterclockwise and $\ddot L = 8.1$ m/s² at this instant, when BC turns at a constant rate.

Answer

(a) $\vec\omega_{OA} = 6\,\hat k$ rad/s, which is 6 rad/s counterclockwise. (b) $\dot L = 1.8$ m/s, so A is moving away from C along the slot.
