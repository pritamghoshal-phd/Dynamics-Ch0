Worked examples

This page collects every example from Chapter 3 of the book, each with a complete step-by-step solution. The examples are grouped by section. Each one has an animation of the system that you can explore, a 3D view of the same motion, and a solution that you can open one step at a time. Try each problem on paper before you open the solution.
