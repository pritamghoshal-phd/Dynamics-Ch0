Example 3.A.6Slotted link driving a pin on a second link

mechanismslotCoriolis

Problem

Link OD is pinned to the wall at O and turns clockwise at the constant rate $\dot\theta = 2$ rad/s, where $\theta$ is measured from the downward vertical. Pin A slides in a slot of link OD and is the end of link CA, which is pinned to the wall at C. Point C is a distance $h$ below O and a horizontal distance $b$ to the left of O. When $\theta = 45°$, link CA is horizontal.

The data are $L = 0.225$ m (the length of CA), $h = 0.225$ m and $b = 0.45$ m.

Find (a) the velocity of A when $\theta = 45°$ and (b) the acceleration of A at the same position.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose the moving frame and find the geometry

Attach the $xyz$ frame to link OD with its origin at the fixed pin O. Then $\vec v_O = \vec a_O = \vec 0$, $\vec\omega = -2\,\hat k$ rad/s and $\vec\alpha = \vec 0$.

Put the origin of the fixed $XY$ axes at O. Then C is at $(-0.45,\ -0.225)$ m. Link CA is horizontal and 0.225 m long, so A is at $(-0.225,\ -0.225)$ m. This point is indeed on the line of the slot at $\theta = 45°$. We write all vectors in fixed $\hat I$, $\hat J$ components.

$$\vec r_{A/O} = -0.225\,\hat I - 0.225\,\hat J\ \text{m}, \qquad \hat e = -\tfrac{1}{\sqrt 2}\left(\hat I + \hat J\right)$$

Here $\hat e$ points along the slot from O toward D, and $d$ is the distance OA. Relative to link OD, A slides along the slot, so $\vec v_{rel} = \dot d\,\hat e$ and $\vec a_{rel} = \ddot d\,\hat e$.

Step 2 · Describe A a second way, as a point of link CA

Link CA turns about the fixed pin C, and $\vec r_{A/C} = L\,\hat I = 0.225\,\hat I$ m.

$$\vec v_A = \omega_{CA}\hat k\times L\,\hat I = L\,\omega_{CA}\,\hat J, \qquad \vec a_A = \alpha_{CA}\hat k\times L\,\hat I - \omega_{CA}^2\,L\,\hat I = -L\,\omega_{CA}^2\,\hat I + L\,\alpha_{CA}\,\hat J$$
Step 3 · Find the velocity of A
$$\vec\omega\times\vec r_{A/O} = (-2\,\hat k)\times(-0.225\,\hat I - 0.225\,\hat J) = -0.45\,\hat I + 0.45\,\hat J\ \text{m/s}$$
$$\vec v_A = -0.45\,\hat I + 0.45\,\hat J - 0.7071\,\dot d\,(\hat I + \hat J) = L\,\omega_{CA}\,\hat J$$
$$\hat I:\ \ -0.45 - 0.7071\,\dot d = 0 \ \Rightarrow\ \dot d = -0.636\ \text{m/s}, \qquad \hat J:\ \ 0.45 + 0.45 = 0.225\,\omega_{CA} \ \Rightarrow\ \omega_{CA} = 4\ \text{rad/s}$$

So A slides toward O along the slot, and $\vec v_A = 0.9\,\hat J$ m/s.

Step 4 · Find the acceleration of A

With $\vec v_{rel} = \dot d\,\hat e = 0.45\,\hat I + 0.45\,\hat J$ m/s, the terms of the moving-frame equation are

$$-\omega^2\,\vec r_{A/O} = -4\,(-0.225\,\hat I - 0.225\,\hat J) = 0.9\,\hat I + 0.9\,\hat J$$
$$2\,\vec\omega\times\vec v_{rel} = 2\,(-2\,\hat k)\times(0.45\,\hat I + 0.45\,\hat J) = 1.8\,\hat I - 1.8\,\hat J, \qquad \vec a_{rel} = -0.7071\,\ddot d\,(\hat I + \hat J)$$

Setting the sum equal to the expression from link CA, with $L\,\omega_{CA}^2 = 0.225(16) = 3.6$ m/s²,

$$\hat I:\ \ 0.9 + 1.8 - 0.7071\,\ddot d = -3.6 \ \Rightarrow\ \ddot d = 8.91\ \text{m/s}^2$$
$$\hat J:\ \ 0.9 - 1.8 - 6.3 = 0.225\,\alpha_{CA} \ \Rightarrow\ \alpha_{CA} = -32\ \text{rad/s}^2$$
$$\vec a_A = -3.6\,\hat I - 7.2\,\hat J\ \text{m/s}^2$$
Answer

(a) $\vec v_A = 0.9\,\hat J$ m/s, straight up. (b) $\vec a_A = -3.6\,\hat I - 7.2\,\hat J$ m/s², with magnitude 8.05 m/s². Link CA turns at 4 rad/s counterclockwise with an angular acceleration of 32 rad/s² clockwise.
