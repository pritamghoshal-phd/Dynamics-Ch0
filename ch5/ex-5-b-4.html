Example 5.B.4Plate swinging about a corner

work–energyfixed-axis rotationparallel axis

Problem

A homogeneous rectangular plate of height $d$ and width $1.5d$ is pinned at its upper right corner O. The plate is released from rest with its top edge horizontal, and the pin is smooth.

Find the largest angular velocity that the plate reaches as it swings about O.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

The plate is acted on by its weight $mg$ at the center G and by the pin reaction at O. The pin does not move, so the pin reaction does no work.

Only the weight $mg$ does work. The pin reaction ($O_x$, $O_y$) acts at the fixed point O. G is a distance $\rho = \tfrac{\sqrt{13}}{4}d$ from O; it is lowest when it passes the dashed vertical line through O.

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equation (work–energy)

Only gravity does work, so mechanical energy is conserved and $T_1 + V_1 = T_2 + V_2$ with $T_1 = 0$. The kinetic energy is largest where the potential energy is smallest, which is when G is directly below O. At that position $\sum M_O = 0$, so $\alpha = 0$ and $\omega$ has stopped increasing.

$$\tfrac12 I_O\,\omega_{max}^2 = m g\,\Delta h$$
Step 3 · Work out the geometry and the moment of inertia

Relative to O, the mass center G lies $0.75d$ to the side and $0.5d$ below. Its distance from O is therefore $\rho = d\sqrt{0.75^2 + 0.5^2} = \tfrac{\sqrt{13}}{4}d$. G starts $0.5d$ below O and ends $\rho$ below O, so it falls through

$$\Delta h = \rho - \tfrac d2 = \tfrac{\sqrt{13} - 2}{4}\,d$$

The parallel-axis theorem gives the moment of inertia about O.

$$I_O = \tfrac1{12}m\left[(1.5d)^2 + d^2\right] + m\rho^2 = \tfrac{13}{48}md^2 + \tfrac{13}{16}md^2 = \tfrac{13}{12}md^2$$
Step 4 · Solve $$\omega_{max}^2 = \frac{2mg\,\Delta h}{I_O} = \frac{6(\sqrt{13} - 2)}{13}\,\frac gd \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \omega_{max} = 0.861\sqrt{g/d}$$

For $d = 1$ m this gives 2.70 rad/s, which matches the statistic in the animation.

Answer

$\omega_{max} = \sqrt{\dfrac{6(\sqrt{13}-2)}{13}\,\dfrac gd} \approx 0.861\sqrt{g/d}$. It is reached when G passes directly below O.
