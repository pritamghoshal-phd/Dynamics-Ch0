Example 5.B.10Wheel pulled up an incline by a band

work–energyrollingcables

Problem

A wheel of weight 25 lb has radius $r = 0.5$ ft and radius of gyration $k_G = 4$ in about its mass center G. It sits on an incline of angle $\theta = 36.87°$. A band is secured at A, near the top of the incline, lies along the incline surface, wraps around the lower side of the wheel and leaves the top of the wheel parallel to the incline. A constant force $F = 20$ lb pulls on the free end of the band. The wheel is released from rest and rolls on the band without slipping.

Find the speed of G when the wheel has rolled 0.8 ft up the incline.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

The wheel is acted on by its weight $W$ at G, the tension $F$ at the top where the band leaves the wheel, and the contact force (normal force and friction) from the band and incline at the bottom contact point C.

The band pulls with $F$ at the top of the wheel, parallel to the incline. At the contact point C the band and incline exert a normal force $N$ and a friction force $f$. C has zero velocity because the wheel rolls without slipping, so $N$ and $f$ do no work. The friction is drawn up the incline, which is its direction here ($f \approx 12$ lb).

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equation (work–energy)

The wheel rolls on the stationary part of the band, so the contact point C has zero velocity and the contact force does no work. The tension $F$ acts at the top of the wheel. The work it does equals $F$ times the distance the band end moves. The weight does negative work because G rises. With the wheel starting from rest,

$$U_{1\to2} = F\,s_{end} - W s\sin\theta = T_2, \qquad T_2 = \tfrac12 m v_G^2 + \tfrac12 m k_G^2\,\omega^2$$
Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

For rolling without slipping, $\omega = v_G/r$. The top of the wheel moves at $2v_G$, so the free end of the band moves twice as far as G. When G moves $s = 0.8$ ft, the band end moves $s_{end} = 1.6$ ft.

$$T_2 = \tfrac12 m v_G^2\left(1 + \frac{k_G^2}{r^2}\right) = \tfrac12\left(\frac{25}{32.2}\right)\left(1 + \frac{(1/3)^2}{(0.5)^2}\right)v_G^2 = 0.5607\,v_G^2$$
Step 4 · Solve $$U_{1\to2} = 20(1.6) - 25(0.8)(0.6) = 32 - 12 = 20\ \text{ft·lb}$$ $$0.5607\,v_G^2 = 20 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad v_G = 5.97\ \text{ft/s}$$

Here $k_G = 4$ in $= 1/3$ ft and $\sin 36.87° = 0.6$.

Answer

$v_G = 5.97$ ft/s up the incline after the wheel has rolled 0.8 ft.
