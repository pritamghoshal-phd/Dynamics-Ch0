Example 5.C.1Drum hoisted by two cable forces

linear impulseangular impulseconstant forces

Problem

A drum of mass $m$ and radius $r$ has a radius of gyration $k_O$ about its center O. A body of mass $M$ hangs from the axle of the drum at O. A cable passes over the top of the drum, and constant forces $F$ and $2F$ pull its left and right ends vertically upward. At time $t = 0$ the center O moves downward at 4 m/s and the drum turns counterclockwise at $\omega_1$.

The data are $\omega_1 = 2$ rad/s, $r = 0.4$ m, $k_O = 0.4$ m, $m = 20$ kg, $M = 30$ kg and $F = 100$ N.

Find (a) the velocity of O and (b) the angular velocity of the drum after 10 s.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

For the translation we take the drum and the hanging body together. The external forces are the two cable forces $F$ and $2F$, both upward, and the total weight $(m + M)g$, downward. The pin force between the drum and the hanging body is internal.

For the rotation we take the drum alone. The cable forces act at the left and right sides of the drum, a distance $r$ from O. The weight of the drum and the force from the hanging body both act at O, so they have no moment about O.

Left: the pin force between the drum and the hanging body is internal, so only $F$, $2F$ and the total weight $(m + M)g$, acting at the mass center G of the system, act. Right: the force $P$ from the hanging body and the weight $mg$ act at O and have no moment about O. Counterclockwise is positive.

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equations (impulse and momentum)

Take upward as positive for the velocity of O and counterclockwise as positive for the rotation. Because the forces are constant, each impulse is a force multiplied by the time interval $\Delta t = 10$ s. Linear impulse and momentum for the whole system gives

$$(m + M)\left(v_{O2} - v_{O1}\right) = \left[3F - (m + M)g\right]\Delta t$$

The force $F$ on the left side turns the drum clockwise and the force $2F$ on the right side turns it counterclockwise, so the net moment about O is $2Fr - Fr = Fr$. Angular impulse and momentum about the mass center O of the drum gives

$$m k_O^2\left(\omega_2 - \omega_1\right) = F r\,\Delta t$$
Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

No kinematic constraint is needed. Both ends of the cable are pulled by known forces, so the rotation of the drum is not tied to the motion of O.

Step 4 · Solve $$v_{O2} = -4 + \frac{3(100) - 50(9.81)}{50}\,(10) = -4 - 38.1 = -42.1\ \text{m/s}$$ $$\omega_2 = 2 + \frac{(100)(0.4)(10)}{20(0.4)^2} = 2 + 125 = 127\ \text{rad/s}$$

The upward pull $3F = 300$ N is smaller than the total weight of 490.5 N, so O keeps speeding up downward. The rotation depends only on the difference between the two cable forces.

Answer

(a) $v_O = 42.1$ m/s downward. (b) $\omega = 127$ rad/s counterclockwise.
