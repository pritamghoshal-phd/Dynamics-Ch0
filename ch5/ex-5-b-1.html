Example 5.B.1Stepped drum after a drop

work–energysystemscables

Problem

A stepped drum of mass $M$ has a radius of gyration $k_O$ about its center O and turns on a smooth shaft. Block A hangs from a cable wrapped around the outer radius $R$ on the left side. Block B hangs from a cable wrapped around the inner radius $r$ on the right side. Neither cable slips, and the system is released from rest.

The data are $m_A = 10$ kg, $m_B = 30$ kg, $M = 20$ kg, $r = 0.2$ m, $R = 0.4$ m and $k_O = 0.25$ m.

Find the speed of block B after it has dropped 1.5 m.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

We take the whole system, consisting of the drum, both blocks and both cables. The external forces are the weights $m_A g$, $m_B g$ and $Mg$ and the pin reaction at O. The cable tensions are internal forces. Each cable is inextensible and does not slip, so the work done at its two ends cancels.

In the system diagram the cables are inside the boundary, so the tensions $T_A$ and $T_B$ do not appear. The separate diagrams show that each tension acts on the drum and on a block with equal and opposite directions, so its work on the two bodies cancels. The pin reactions $O_x$ and $O_y$ act at a point that does not move, so they do no work.

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equation (work–energy)

The system starts from rest, so $T_1 = 0$ and the work–energy principle reduces to $U_{1\to2} = T_2$. The pin reaction and the weight of the drum act at the fixed point O, so they do no work. Only the weights of the two blocks do work.

$$U_{1\to2} = m_B g\,s_B - m_A g\,s_A, \qquad T_2 = \tfrac12 m_A v_A^2 + \tfrac12 m_B v_B^2 + \tfrac12 M k_O^2\,\omega^2$$
Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

Because the cables do not slip, $v_A = R\omega$ and $v_B = r\omega$, and the displacements are related in the same way. When B drops $s_B = 1.5$ m, the drum turns through $\theta = s_B/r = 7.5$ rad, and A rises $s_A = R\theta = 3$ m.

Step 4 · Solve $$U_{1\to2} = 30(9.81)(1.5) - 10(9.81)(3) = 441.5 - 294.3 = 147.2\ \text{J}$$ $$T_2 = \tfrac12\left(m_A R^2 + m_B r^2 + M k_O^2\right)\omega^2 = \tfrac12(4.05)\,\omega^2$$

Setting $T_2 = U_{1\to2}$ gives $\omega = \sqrt{2(147.2)/4.05} = 8.52$ rad/s, and therefore $v_B = r\omega = 1.70$ m/s.

This agrees with Section A. The effective moment of inertia and the net moment are the same as in Example 5.A.11, so $a_B = r\alpha = 0.969$ m/s² and $v_B = \sqrt{2a_B s_B} = 1.70$ m/s.

Answer

$v_B = 1.70$ m/s downward, and the drum turns at $\omega = 8.52$ rad/s.
