Example 5.C.2Spool pulled by a falling block

impulse–momentumrollingcables

Problem

A spool of mass $M$ has a radius of gyration $k_O$ about its center O. Its outer rims, of radius $R$, roll without slipping on a rough horizontal floor. A cable is wrapped around the hub of radius $r$. It leaves the top of the hub horizontally, passes over an ideal pulley at the edge of the floor and supports block A of mass $m$. The cable does not slip on the hub. The system is released from rest.

The data are $m = 10$ kg, $M = 20$ kg, $r = 0.2$ m, $R = 0.4$ m and $k_O = 0.25$ m.

Find the angular velocity of the spool after 10 s.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

The spool is acted on by its weight $Mg$, the normal force $N$ and the friction force $F_f$ at the contact point, and the cable tension $T$, which pulls horizontally toward the pulley at the top of the hub. Block A is acted on by the tension $T$ upward and its weight $mg$ downward. The ideal pulley does not change the tension.

The cable leaves the top of the hub, a distance $r$ above O, and pulls toward the pulley on the right. The friction force $F_f$ is drawn in the positive direction, toward the pulley; the solution finds its true sign. The ideal pulley passes the tension $T$ on unchanged.

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equations (impulse and momentum)

All the forces are constant, so the motion has constant accelerations and each impulse equals a force multiplied by $t$. Take the motion of O toward the pulley as positive, the rotation of the spool as positive when it is clockwise (the direction of rolling toward the pulley), and the motion of A as positive downward. Starting from rest,

$$M v_O = (T + F_f)\,t, \qquad M k_O^2\,\omega = (T r - F_f R)\,t, \qquad m v_A = (m g - T)\,t$$

The first two equations are the linear and angular impulse–momentum equations of the spool, with moments taken about its mass center O. The third is the linear impulse–momentum equation of block A.

Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

The spool rolls without slipping, so $v_O = R\,\omega$. The cable leaves the top of the hub, whose velocity is $v_O + r\omega = (R + r)\,\omega$. The cable is inextensible, so block A moves at the same speed, $v_A = (R + r)\,\omega$.

Step 4 · Solve

Multiply the first spool equation by $R$ and add it to the second to eliminate the friction impulse. This gives $M(k_O^2 + R^2)\,\omega = T(R + r)\,t$. Then eliminate $T\,t$ with the equation of block A.

$$\omega = \frac{m g\,(R + r)\,t}{M\left(k_O^2 + R^2\right) + m\,(R + r)^2} = \frac{10(9.81)(0.6)(10)}{20(0.0625 + 0.16) + 10(0.36)} = \frac{588.6}{8.05} = 73.1\ \text{rad/s}$$

The tension is $T = 54.2$ N and the friction force on the spool is 4.27 N toward the pulley, so only a small friction coefficient is needed for rolling.

Answer

$\omega = 73.1$ rad/s clockwise, which is the direction in which the spool rolls toward the pulley.
