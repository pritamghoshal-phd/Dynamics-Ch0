Example 2.A.9Links driven by a hydraulic cylinder

two linksvelocity equationacceleration equation

Problem

Link OB, of length 500 mm, turns about a fixed pin O. Link AB, of length 300 mm, connects B to pin A at the end of the piston rod of a hydraulic cylinder C. Pin A moves along the vertical line through O. The piston rod gives pin A a constant acceleration $a_A = 0.1$ m/s² downward. At the instant when the angle $\theta$ between the vertical and AB is 90°, the speed of A is $v_A = 0.4$ m/s upward.

Find, at this instant, (a) the angular velocity of link AB, (b) the angular velocity of link OB, (c) the angular acceleration of link AB and (d) the angular acceleration of link OB.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw a kinematics diagram

Put the origin at A, with $\hat{\mathbf i}$ to the right, $\hat{\mathbf j}$ upward and $\hat{\mathbf k}$ out of the page. At $\theta = 90°$ link AB is horizontal, so $\mathbf r_{B/A} = 0.3\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ m. Point O is on the vertical line through A, and $OB = 0.5$ m, so O is $\sqrt{0.5^2 - 0.3^2} = 0.4$ m above A and

$$\mathbf r_{B/O} = 0.3\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.4\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{m}$$

The motion of A is given, $\mathbf v_A = 0.4\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s and $\mathbf a_A = -0.1\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s². The four unknowns are $\omega_{AB}$, $\omega_{OB}$, $\alpha_{AB}$ and $\alpha_{OB}$, all drawn as counterclockwise in the diagram.

AB = 0.3 m and OB = 0.5 m, so O is 0.4 m above A because OB, AB and OA form a 3–4–5 triangle at this instant. The unknown rotations are drawn counterclockwise (positive). The given acceleration of A points down while A moves up.

Step 2 · Write the velocity equations

The velocity of B can be written from link AB and from link OB.

$$\mathbf v_B = \mathbf v_A + \omega_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{B/A} = 0.4\,\hat{\mathbf j} + 0.3\,\omega_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$ $$\mathbf v_B = \omega_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{B/O} = 0.4\,\omega_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.3\,\omega_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$

The $\hat{\mathbf i}$ components give $0.4\,\omega_{OB} = 0$, so $\omega_{OB} = 0$. The $\hat{\mathbf j}$ components then give $0.4 + 0.3\,\omega_{AB} = 0$, so $\omega_{AB} = -1.333$ rad/s.

At this instant B is momentarily at rest. Link AB turns about B, which is its instant center.

Step 3 · Write the acceleration equations

From link AB,

$$\mathbf a_B = \mathbf a_A + \alpha_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{B/A} - \omega_{AB}^2\,\mathbf r_{B/A} = -0.1\,\hat{\mathbf j} + 0.3\,\alpha_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf j} - (1.333)^2(0.3)\,\hat{\mathbf i}$$ $$\mathbf a_B = -0.533\,\hat{\mathbf i} + (0.3\,\alpha_{AB} - 0.1)\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$

From link OB, whose angular velocity is zero,

$$\mathbf a_B = \alpha_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{B/O} = 0.4\,\alpha_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.3\,\alpha_{OB}\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$
Step 4 · Solve

The $\hat{\mathbf i}$ components give $0.4\,\alpha_{OB} = -0.533$, so $\alpha_{OB} = -1.333$ rad/s². The $\hat{\mathbf j}$ components give $0.3\,\alpha_{OB} = 0.3\,\alpha_{AB} - 0.1$, so $-0.4 = 0.3\,\alpha_{AB} - 0.1$ and $\alpha_{AB} = -1.000$ rad/s².

Link OB has zero angular velocity but a clockwise angular acceleration, so it is just starting to turn clockwise. The plot in the animation shows $\omega_{OB}$ crossing zero at the instant.

Answer

(a) $\boldsymbol\omega_{AB} = -1.333\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s. (b) $\boldsymbol\omega_{OB} = \mathbf 0$. (c) $\boldsymbol\alpha_{AB} = -1.000\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s². (d) $\boldsymbol\alpha_{OB} = -1.333\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s². All nonzero rotations are clockwise.
