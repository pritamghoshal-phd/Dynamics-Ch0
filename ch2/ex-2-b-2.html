Example 2.B.2Pulley climbing a fixed cable

instant centerspulleysno slip

Problem

A cable wrapped around the inner radius $r$ of the pulley shown is pulled upward at the rate $v_A$. A second cable is wrapped around the outer radius $R$, and its upper end is attached to the ceiling. The pulley does not slip on either cable.

The data are $v_A = 3$ m/s, $r = 0.5$ ft and $R = 1$ ft.

Find (a) the location of the instant center of the pulley, and (b) the velocity of point B on the outer radius at the instant when B is directly above the center O. Sketch this velocity vector.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center. The book mixes units (m/s for the cable speed and ft for the radii). We convert the radii to metres: $r = 0.1524$ m and $R = 0.3048$ m. The velocity of B turns out not to depend on this choice, because it involves only the ratio of the radii.

Step 1 · Draw the kinematics diagram

The left side of the pulley touches the fixed cable at point C. The right side of the inner radius is in contact with the pulled cable at the point labeled A in the diagram.

Point C of the pulley touches the fixed cable without slipping, so its velocity is zero. Every other point moves perpendicular to its line from C.

Step 2 · Locate the instant center

The pulley does not slip on the fixed cable, and that cable does not move. The point C of the pulley that touches it therefore has zero velocity, so C is the instant center.

C lies on the horizontal diameter, a distance $R$ to the left of O.

Step 3 · Find the angular velocity

The point of the pulley that touches the pulled cable moves with that cable, at $v_A$ upward. It is a distance $R + r$ from C, so

$$\omega = \frac{v_A}{R + r} = \frac{3}{0.3048 + 0.1524} = 6.56\ \text{rad/s}$$

This point is to the right of C and moves up, so the pulley turns counterclockwise. The center O, a distance $R$ from C, rises at $v_O = \omega R = 2.00$ m/s.

Step 4 · Find the velocity of B

When B is directly above O, its position relative to C is $\mathbf r_{B/C} = R\,\hat{\mathbf i} + R\,\hat{\mathbf j}$. Its distance from C is $\sqrt2\,R$, and its velocity is perpendicular to the line CB.

$$\mathbf v_B = \omega\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{B/C} = \omega R\left(-\hat{\mathbf i} + \hat{\mathbf j}\right) = -2.00\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 2.00\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{m/s}$$ $$v_B = \sqrt2\,\omega R = \frac{\sqrt2\,R}{R + r}\,v_A = 2.83\ \text{m/s}$$

The velocity of B points up and to the left at 45°.

Answer

(a) The instant center is the point C where the pulley touches the fixed cable. (b) $\mathbf v_B = -2.00\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 2.00\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s, a speed of 2.83 m/s directed up and to the left at 45°. The pulley turns counterclockwise at 6.56 rad/s.
