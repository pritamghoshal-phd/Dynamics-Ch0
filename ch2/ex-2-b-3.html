Example 2.B.3Spool pulled by a cable from its hub

instant centersrolling without slippingspool

Problem

A cable is wrapped around the inner radius $r$ of a spool. End A of the cable moves to the right with speed $v_A$, and the cable leaves the bottom of the hub horizontally. The outer radius $R$ of the spool rolls without slipping on a rough horizontal surface.

The data are $v_A = 0.8$ m/s, $r = 0.3$ m and $R = 0.9$ m.

Find, using the method of instant centers, (a) the velocity of the center O of the spool and (b) the angular velocity of the spool.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center.

Step 1 · Draw the kinematics diagram

Call P the point of the hub where the cable leaves it. P is at the bottom of the hub, a height $R - r$ above the contact point C. Because the cable does not slip on the hub, P moves with the cable, $v_P = v_A$ to the right.

The velocities along the vertical line through C grow linearly with the height above C (dashed line), because every point turns about C at the same rate ω.

Step 2 · Locate the instant center

The spool rolls without slipping on the fixed ground, so the contact point C has zero velocity. C is the instant center of the spool.

Step 3 · Find the angular velocity

P is directly above C at a distance $R - r = 0.6$ m, so

$$\omega = \frac{v_P}{R - r} = \frac{0.8}{0.6} = 1.33\ \text{rad/s}$$

P is above C and moves to the right, so the spool turns clockwise.

Step 4 · Find the velocity of the center

O is a distance $R$ above C, so

$$v_O = \omega R = 1.33(0.9) = 1.20\ \text{m/s, to the right}$$

The center moves faster than the cable end, 1.2 m/s compared with 0.8 m/s. The horizontal length of cable between the spool and A therefore shrinks, which means the cable winds onto the hub as the spool rolls toward A.

Answer

(a) $v_O = 1.20$ m/s to the right. (b) $\omega = 1.33$ rad/s clockwise.
