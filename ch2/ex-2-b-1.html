Example 2.B.1Instant center of a four-bar coupler

instant centersfour-bar linkageangular velocity

Problem

Link OA rotates about pin O with an angular speed of $\omega_{OA} = 3$ rad/s in the counterclockwise sense. At the instant shown, link OA is horizontal, link AB is vertical, and link DB makes the angle $\theta = 36.87°$ with AB. Links OA and AB both have length $L$, and the fixed pin D lies on the horizontal line through O and A.

Find (a) the location of the instant center $IC_{AB}$ of link AB, and (b) the angular velocities of links AB and DB, using the location of $IC_{AB}$.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center.

Step 1 · Find the directions of the velocities of A and B

Point A belongs to link OA, which turns about the fixed pin O. Its velocity is therefore perpendicular to OA. With OA horizontal and turning counterclockwise, $\mathbf v_A$ points straight up and has magnitude

$$v_A = \omega_{OA} L = 3L$$

Point B belongs to link DB, which turns about the fixed pin D. Its velocity is therefore perpendicular to DB.

$\mathbf v_A$ is perpendicular to OA, so the instant center of AB lies on line OA. $\mathbf v_B$ is perpendicular to DB, so it also lies on line DB. The two lines meet at D.

Step 2 · Locate the instant center of AB

The instant center of AB lies on the line through A perpendicular to $\mathbf v_A$, which is the horizontal line OA. It also lies on the line through B perpendicular to $\mathbf v_B$, which is line DB itself. These two lines meet at D, so

$$IC_{AB} = \text{point D}$$

From the geometry, D is a horizontal distance $L\tan\theta = 0.75L$ to the right of A, and the length of DB is $L/\cos\theta = 1.25L$.

Step 3 · Find the angular velocity of AB

Every point of AB moves as if AB were turning about D at this instant. Point A is $0.75L$ from D and moves up at $3L$, so

$$\omega_{AB} = \frac{v_A}{r_{A/IC}} = \frac{3L}{0.75L} = 4\ \text{rad/s}$$

A lies to the left of D and moves upward, so AB turns clockwise.

Step 4 · Find the angular velocity of DB

Point B is $1.25L$ from the instant center of AB, so its speed is

$$v_B = \omega_{AB}\, r_{B/IC} = 4(1.25L) = 5L$$

Point B is also $1.25L$ from the fixed pin D of link DB, so

$$\omega_{DB} = \frac{v_B}{L_{DB}} = \frac{5L}{1.25L} = 4\ \text{rad/s, clockwise}$$

The two angular velocities are equal at this instant because the pivot D of DB and the instant center of AB are the same point. Check with vectors. The result has magnitude $5L$ and is perpendicular to DB.

$$\mathbf v_B = \mathbf v_A + \boldsymbol\omega_{AB}\times\mathbf r_{B/A} = 3L\,\hat{\mathbf j} + (-4\hat{\mathbf k})\times(-L\hat{\mathbf j}) = -4L\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 3L\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$
Answer

(a) The instant center of AB is at the fixed pin D. (b) $\omega_{AB} = 4$ rad/s clockwise and $\omega_{DB} = 4$ rad/s clockwise. Neither answer depends on $L$.
