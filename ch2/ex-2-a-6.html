Example 2.A.6Spool pulled by a cable from its hub

rolling without slippingvelocityinstant center

Problem

A cable is wrapped around the inner hub, of radius $r$, of a spool whose outer radius is $R$. The cable leaves the bottom of the hub horizontally, and its end A moves to the right with speed $v_A$. The spool rolls without slipping on a rough horizontal surface, and C is its point of contact with the surface.

The data are $v_A = 2$ m/s, $r = 0.4$ m and $R = 0.8$ m.

Find (a) the velocity of the center O of the spool and (b) the angular velocity of the spool. Express both answers as vectors.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw a kinematics diagram

Take $\hat{\mathbf i}$ to the right, $\hat{\mathbf j}$ upward and $\hat{\mathbf k}$ out of the page, so that a counterclockwise rotation is positive. Let P be the point of the hub where the cable leaves it, directly below O. The cable is inextensible and does not slip on the hub, so P moves with the cable, $\mathbf v_P = v_A\,\hat{\mathbf i}$.

The spool rolls without slipping, so its contact point C has zero velocity. The position of P relative to C is $\mathbf r_{P/C} = (R - r)\,\hat{\mathbf j} = 0.4\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m, and the position of O relative to C is $\mathbf r_{O/C} = R\,\hat{\mathbf j} = 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m.

The contact point C has zero velocity. The cable point P moves with the cable at $v_A$, and the velocities of P and O grow in proportion to their heights above C (thin red line). The rotation $\omega$ is drawn clockwise, the direction it turns out to have.

Step 2 · Find the angular velocity from point P

Apply the rigid-body velocity equation between C and P.

$$\mathbf v_P = \mathbf v_C + \boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r_{P/C}$$ $$2\,\hat{\mathbf i} = \mathbf 0 + \omega\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times 0.4\,\hat{\mathbf j} = -0.4\,\omega\,\hat{\mathbf i}$$

The $\hat{\mathbf i}$ components give $\omega = -2/0.4 = -5$ rad/s. The negative sign means that the spool turns clockwise.

Step 3 · Find the velocity of the center O

Apply the same equation between C and O.

$$\mathbf v_O = \mathbf v_C + \boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r_{O/C} = (-5\,\hat{\mathbf k})\times 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf j} = 4\,\hat{\mathbf i}\ \text{m/s}$$

The center moves at 4 m/s while the cable end moves at only 2 m/s. The spool therefore rolls toward A and winds up cable at $4 - 2 = 2$ m/s. Points of the spool above O move even faster, and the top point moves at $2R|\omega| = 8$ m/s.

Answer

(a) $\mathbf v_O = 4\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ m/s. (b) $\boldsymbol\omega = -5\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s, which is 5 rad/s clockwise.

Explore

  • Move the hub radius $r$ close to $R$. Why does the spool then move much faster than the cable?
  • Suppose the cable left the top of the hub instead. Use the same method to show that $v_O = v_A R/(R + r)$, which is slower than the cable.
