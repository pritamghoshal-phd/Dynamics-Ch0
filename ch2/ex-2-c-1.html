Example 2.C.1Rolling wheel driven by a rod and a slider

mechanismrolling without slippingacceleration equation

Problem

A wheel of radius $r$ rolls without slipping on a horizontal surface. Point A on its rim is pinned to rod AB of length $L$, and end B of the rod is pinned to a slider. The slider moves to the left with a constant speed $v_B = 5$ ft/s, and pin B stays level with the contact point of the wheel. The angle $\theta$ locates A and is measured counterclockwise from the horizontal line through the center O, on the side of the slider.

The data are $L = 2$ ft and $r = 0.5$ ft.

Find, when $\theta = 0$, (a) the angular velocity of the wheel and (b) the angular acceleration of the wheel.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw a kinematics diagram

Put the origin at the contact point C, with $\hat{\mathbf i}$ to the right, $\hat{\mathbf j}$ upward and $\hat{\mathbf k}$ out of the page. When $\theta = 0$, the center is at $O = (0, 0.5)$ ft and A is level with O at $(0.5, 0.5)$ ft. Pin B is on the line of the contact point and 2 ft from A, so it is at $\left(0.5 + \sqrt{2^2 - 0.5^2},\ 0\right) = (2.436, 0)$ ft. The relative positions are

$$\mathbf r_{A/O} = 0.5\,\hat{\mathbf i}, \qquad \mathbf r_{B/A} = 1.936\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.5\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{ft}$$

The wheel rolls without slipping, so $\mathbf v_O = -r\omega\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ and $\mathbf a_O = -r\alpha\,\hat{\mathbf i}$, where $\omega$ and $\alpha$ are the counterclockwise angular velocity and acceleration of the wheel. The slider gives $\mathbf v_B = -5\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ ft/s and $\mathbf a_B = \mathbf 0$.

The contact point C is the instant center of the wheel. The rotations $\omega$ and $\omega_{AB}$ are drawn counterclockwise (positive), and $\mathbf v_O = -r\omega\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ follows from rolling without slipping. The slider moves at constant velocity, so $\mathbf a_B = \mathbf 0$.

Step 2 · Find the velocity of A $$\mathbf v_A = \mathbf v_O + \omega\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{A/O} = -0.5\,\omega\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.5\,\omega\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$
Step 3 · Find the angular velocities

Write the velocity of B from rod AB.

$$-5\,\hat{\mathbf i} = \mathbf v_A + \omega_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times(1.936\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.5\,\hat{\mathbf j}) = (-0.5\,\omega + 0.5\,\omega_{AB})\,\hat{\mathbf i} + (0.5\,\omega + 1.936\,\omega_{AB})\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$

The $\hat{\mathbf j}$ components give $\omega_{AB} = -0.2582\,\omega$. Substituting into the $\hat{\mathbf i}$ components gives $-5 = -0.5\,\omega - 0.1291\,\omega$, so

$$\omega = 7.95\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \omega_{AB} = -2.05\ \text{rad/s}$$

The wheel turns counterclockwise, which means it rolls to the left and follows the slider.

Step 4 · Find the acceleration of A $$\mathbf a_A = \mathbf a_O + \alpha\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{A/O} - \omega^2\,\mathbf r_{A/O} = -0.5\,\alpha\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.5\,\alpha\,\hat{\mathbf j} - (7.95)^2(0.5)\,\hat{\mathbf i}$$ $$\mathbf a_A = (-0.5\,\alpha - 31.58)\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.5\,\alpha\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$
Step 5 · Find the angular accelerations

Write the acceleration of B from rod AB, with $\mathbf a_B = \mathbf 0$ and $\omega_{AB}^2 = 4.211$ rad²/s².

$$\mathbf 0 = \mathbf a_A + \alpha_{AB}\,\hat{\mathbf k}\times(1.936\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.5\,\hat{\mathbf j}) - 4.211\,(1.936\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.5\,\hat{\mathbf j})$$

The two components give

$$0 = -0.5\,\alpha - 31.58 + 0.5\,\alpha_{AB} - 8.15, \qquad 0 = 0.5\,\alpha + 1.936\,\alpha_{AB} + 2.11$$

Solving these two equations together gives $\alpha = -64.0$ rad/s² and $\alpha_{AB} = 15.4$ rad/s².

The wheel turns counterclockwise but is slowing down, so its angular acceleration is clockwise. The plots in the animation show $\omega$ falling as A rises above the level of O.

Answer

(a) $\boldsymbol\omega = 7.95\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s (counterclockwise). (b) $\boldsymbol\alpha = -64.0\,\hat{\mathbf k}$ rad/s² (clockwise).
