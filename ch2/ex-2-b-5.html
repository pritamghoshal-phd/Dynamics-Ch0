Example 2.B.5Hydraulic cylinder driving a rocker

instant centerslinkagehydraulic cylinder

Problem

The piston rod of the hydraulic cylinder C gives pin A an upward speed $v_A = 4$ m/s. Pin A moves along the vertical line through the fixed pin O. Link OB has length 400 mm and link AB has length 250 mm. The angle $\theta$ is measured at A between the vertical line AO and link AB.

Find, for the instant when $\theta = 45°$, the angular velocity of link OB, using the method of instant centers.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center.

Step 1 · Find the geometry and the velocity directions

Place O at the origin with $y$ upward, and let A be a distance $d$ below O. With $\theta = 45°$, B is $0.25\sin 45° = 0.1768$ m to the right of A and $0.1768$ m above it. The length of OB fixes $d$.

$$0.1768^2 + (d - 0.1768)^2 = 0.4^2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad d = 0.5356\ \text{m}$$

So A is at $(0, -0.5356)$ m and B is at $(0.1768, -0.3588)$ m. The velocity of A is vertical. B belongs to link OB, which turns about O, so $\mathbf v_B$ is perpendicular to OB.

$\mathbf v_A$ is vertical, so the instant center of AB lies on the horizontal line through A. $\mathbf v_B$ is perpendicular to OB, so it also lies on line OB extended.

Step 2 · Locate the instant center of AB

The instant center lies on the horizontal line through A, $y = -0.5356$ m. It also lies on line OB extended, whose points are $s(0.1768, -0.3588)$. Setting $-0.3588s = -0.5356$ gives $s = 1.493$, so

$$IC_{AB} = (0.2639,\ -0.5356)\ \text{m}$$

Its distance from A is $r_{A/IC} = 0.2639$ m. Its distance from B is $(s - 1)\,L_{OB} = 0.493(0.4) = 0.1971$ m.

Step 3 · Find the angular velocity of AB

A moves up at 4 m/s and lies to the left of the instant center, so AB turns clockwise with

$$\omega_{AB} = \frac{v_A}{r_{A/IC}} = \frac{4}{0.2639} = 15.16\ \text{rad/s}$$
Step 4 · Find the angular velocity of OB

The speed of B follows from its distance to the instant center of AB.

$$v_B = \omega_{AB}\, r_{B/IC} = 15.16(0.1971) = 2.987\ \text{m/s}$$

B moves on a circle of radius 0.4 m about O, so

$$\omega_{OB} = \frac{v_B}{L_{OB}} = \frac{2.987}{0.4} = 7.47\ \text{rad/s}$$

B moves up and to the right, perpendicular to OB, which is a counterclockwise rotation of OB about O.

Answer

$\omega_{OB} = 7.47$ rad/s counterclockwise. Along the way, $\omega_{AB} = 15.2$ rad/s clockwise and $v_B = 2.99$ m/s.
