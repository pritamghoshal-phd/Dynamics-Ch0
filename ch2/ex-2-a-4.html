Example 2.A.4Compound gear between two racks

rolling without slipvelocityacceleration

Problem

A compound gear has an inner gear of radius $r = 0.1$ m and an outer gear of radius $R = 0.16$ m. Rack A meshes with the top of the inner gear and moves to the right with speed $v_A = 0.8$ m/s and acceleration $a_A = 2$ m/s². Rack B meshes with the bottom of the outer gear and moves to the left with a constant speed $v_B = 0.6$ m/s. Neither rack slips on the gear.

Find (a) the velocity and (b) the acceleration of point P on the outer rim, level with O and to its right.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the kinematics diagram

Because the racks do not slip, the gear point touching rack A has the velocity of rack A, and the gear point touching rack B has the velocity of rack B. These two points are on the same vertical diameter, a distance $R + r = 0.26$ m apart.

The gear points that touch the racks have the velocities of the racks. Along the vertical diameter the velocity changes linearly from $v_A$ at the top contact to $v_B$ at the bottom contact, and it is zero slightly below O because the gear turns clockwise.

Step 2 · Angular velocity and the velocity of O

Write the velocities of the two contact points with $\mathbf v = \mathbf v_O + \omega\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r$ and $\mathbf v_O = v_O\,\hat{\mathbf i}$.

$$\text{top } \left(\mathbf r = 0.1\,\hat{\mathbf j}\right):\quad v_O - 0.1\,\omega = 0.8, \qquad \text{bottom } \left(\mathbf r = -0.16\,\hat{\mathbf j}\right):\quad v_O + 0.16\,\omega = -0.6$$

Subtracting the first equation from the second gives $0.26\,\omega = -1.4$.

$$\omega = -5.385\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad v_O = 0.8 + 0.1\,\omega = 0.2615\ \text{m/s}$$
Step 3 · Velocity of P $$\mathbf v_P = \mathbf v_O + \omega\hat{\mathbf k}\times\left(0.16\,\hat{\mathbf i}\right) = 0.2615\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.8615\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{m/s}$$
Step 4 · Angular acceleration and the acceleration of O

Rolling without slip also fixes the tangential (horizontal) accelerations of the contact points, which equal the rack accelerations. Point O moves on a horizontal line, so $\mathbf a_O = a_O\,\hat{\mathbf i}$.

$$\text{top}:\quad a_O - 0.1\,\alpha = 2, \qquad \text{bottom}:\quad a_O + 0.16\,\alpha = 0$$ $$\alpha = -\frac{2}{0.26} = -7.692\ \text{rad/s}^2, \qquad a_O = -0.16\,\alpha = 1.231\ \text{m/s}^2$$

The contact points also have vertical accelerations, which are not equal to those of the racks. Only the components along the racks are matched.

Step 5 · Acceleration of P $$\mathbf a_P = \mathbf a_O + \alpha\hat{\mathbf k}\times\mathbf r_{P/O} - \omega^2\,\mathbf r_{P/O} = 1.231\,\hat{\mathbf i} + (-7.692)(0.16)\,\hat{\mathbf j} - (5.385)^2(0.16)\,\hat{\mathbf i}$$ $$\mathbf a_P = -3.41\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 1.23\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{m/s}^2$$
Answer

(a) $\mathbf v_P = 0.262\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.862\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s. (b) $\mathbf a_P = -3.41\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 1.23\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s².
