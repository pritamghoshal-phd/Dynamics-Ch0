Example 2.B.6Two links between two walls

instant centerstwo linkssliding

Problem

Link AB and link BC, each of length $L$, are pinned together at B, which slides along the floor. End A slides along the left wall and end C slides along the right wall. End A moves down with speed $v_A$. Link AB makes the angle $\phi$ with the floor, and link BC makes the angle $\theta$ with the floor.

The data are $\theta = 53.13°$, $\phi = 36.87°$, $L = 0.4$ m and $v_A = 30$ m/s.

Find the speed of end C, using the method of instant centers.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center.

Step 1 · Find the velocity directions

A and C slide along vertical walls, so $\mathbf v_A$ and $\mathbf v_C$ are vertical. B slides along the floor, so $\mathbf v_B$ is horizontal. Point B belongs to both links, and this shared velocity connects the two instant-center calculations.

A and C slide along the walls, so their velocities are vertical. B slides along the floor, so its velocity is horizontal. Both instant centers lie on the vertical line through B.

Step 2 · Find the angular velocity of AB and the velocity of B

The instant center of AB is level with A and directly above B. Its distances are

$$r_{A/IC_{AB}} = L\cos\phi = 0.32\ \text{m}, \qquad r_{B/IC_{AB}} = L\sin\phi = 0.24\ \text{m}$$ $$\omega_{AB} = \frac{v_A}{L\cos\phi} = \frac{30}{0.32} = 93.75\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad v_B = \omega_{AB} L\sin\phi = 93.75(0.24) = 22.5\ \text{m/s}$$

A is to the left of the instant center and moves down, so AB turns counterclockwise and B, below the instant center, moves to the right.

Step 3 · Find the angular velocity of BC

The instant center of BC is level with C and directly above B, so

$$r_{B/IC_{BC}} = L\sin\theta = 0.32\ \text{m}, \qquad r_{C/IC_{BC}} = L\cos\theta = 0.24\ \text{m}$$ $$\omega_{BC} = \frac{v_B}{L\sin\theta} = \frac{22.5}{0.32} = 70.31\ \text{rad/s, counterclockwise}$$
Step 4 · Find the speed of C $$v_C = \omega_{BC}\, L\cos\theta = 70.31(0.24) = 16.9\ \text{m/s}$$

C is to the right of its instant center and BC turns counterclockwise, so C moves up the right wall.

Combining the steps gives the compact result $v_C = v_A\,\dfrac{\tan\phi}{\tan\theta} = 30\,\dfrac{0.75}{1.333} = 16.9$ m/s.

Answer

$v_C = 16.9$ m/s, directed up the right wall.
