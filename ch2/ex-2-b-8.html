Example 2.B.8Motion blur on a racing tire

instant centersrollingreal life

Problem

A photo of the Purdue Formula SAE car on a test track shows the lettering on the right rear tire blurred in what might seem an arbitrary way. If semicircles centered on the point where the tire touches the roadway are drawn over the image, the blur of the lettering lines up with these semicircles.

Explain this observation. Hint: consider the location of the instant center of the tire, assuming that the tire does not slip on the roadway.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center.

Step 1 · Locate the instant center of the tire

The tire rolls without slipping, so the point of the tire touching the road has the same velocity as the road, which is zero. That contact point C is the instant center of the tire.

Every point of the tire moves perpendicular to its line from C, with a speed proportional to its distance from C. During a short exposure each mark therefore sweeps a short arc of a circle centered at C.

Step 2 · Describe the velocity of every point

At any instant, every point P of the tire moves as if the tire were turning about C.

$$\mathbf v_P = \boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r_{P/C}, \qquad v_P = \omega\, r_{P/C}$$

So each point moves perpendicular to its line from C, with a speed proportional to its distance from C. The top of the tire moves at $2v$, the center at $v$, and the contact point not at all.

Step 3 · Relate the velocity to the blur

During a short exposure $\Delta t$, a mark on the tire moves a distance of about $\mathbf v_P\,\Delta t$. That displacement is perpendicular to the line from C, so it is tangent to the circle centered at C through the mark. The blurred streak therefore lies along that circle.

The length of the streak is $\omega\, r_{P/C}\,\Delta t$. Marks far from the road, near the top of the tire, leave long streaks, and marks near the road leave short ones. This matches the photo.

Step 4 · Note the role of the camera

The photo was taken with the camera fixed relative to the ground, so it records velocities relative to the ground. Relative to the car, every point of the tire moves on a circle about the axle O, so a camera mounted on the car would show blur along circles about O instead. Try both camera options in the animation.

Answer

The contact point C is the instant center of the tire. Every point moves perpendicular to its line from C at a speed proportional to its distance from C. During the exposure each letter is therefore smeared along a short arc of a circle centered at C, and the smear is longest near the top of the tire.

Explore

  • Increase the exposure time. When do the streaks stop looking like arcs about C, and why?
  • Switch to the camera moving with the car. Why are the streaks now the same length all around the tire?
