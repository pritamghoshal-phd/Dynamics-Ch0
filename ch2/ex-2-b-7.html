Example 2.B.7Link with ends on a 45° guide and a level track

instant centersguidesmass center

Problem

The roller at end A of link AB moves in a straight guide inclined at the angle $\theta$ to the horizontal. The roller at end B moves along a horizontal track with speed $v_B$ to the right. The link has length $L$, makes the angle $\phi$ with the horizontal, and G is its midpoint.

The data are $\theta = 45°$, $\phi = 30°$, $L = 0.6$ m and $v_B = 2.5$ m/s.

Find, using the method of instant centers, (a) the angular velocity of link AB, (b) the speed of end A and (c) the speed of G.

Solution

The method of instant centers has four steps. Find the direction of the velocity of two points of the body, locate the instant center where the perpendiculars to those velocities meet, find ω from one known speed, and then find any other velocity from its distance to the instant center. The figure in the book draws the guide at A less steep than the link, while the data give a 45° guide and a 30° link. We follow the data, so the guide is drawn as a slot in a plate behind the link.

Step 1 · Set up the geometry

Put A at the origin. B is then at $L(\cos\phi, \sin\phi) = (0.5196, 0.3)$ m, and G is at $(0.2598, 0.15)$ m. The velocity of A points along the guide, at 45°, and the velocity of B is horizontal.

$\mathbf v_A$ points along the 45° guide and $\mathbf v_B$ is horizontal. The perpendicular to $\mathbf v_A$ at A and the vertical line through B meet at the instant center.

Step 2 · Locate the instant center

The perpendicular to $\mathbf v_A$ at A is the line through the origin at 135°, whose points are $s(-1, 1)$. The perpendicular to $\mathbf v_B$ at B is the vertical line $x = 0.5196$ m. They meet at $s = -0.5196$, so

$$IC_{AB} = (0.5196,\ -0.5196)\ \text{m}$$ $$r_{B/IC} = 0.3 + 0.5196 = 0.8196\ \text{m}, \qquad r_{A/IC} = 0.5196\sqrt2 = 0.7348\ \text{m}$$ $$r_{G/IC} = \sqrt{0.2598^2 + 0.6696^2} = 0.7183\ \text{m}$$
Step 3 · Find the angular velocity of AB

B lies directly above the instant center and moves to the right, so the link turns clockwise with

$$\omega_{AB} = \frac{v_B}{r_{B/IC}} = \frac{2.5}{0.8196} = 3.05\ \text{rad/s}$$
Step 4 · Find the speeds of A and G $$v_A = \omega_{AB}\, r_{A/IC} = 3.05(0.7348) = 2.24\ \text{m/s}$$ $$v_G = \omega_{AB}\, r_{G/IC} = 3.05(0.7183) = 2.19\ \text{m/s}$$

A moves up the guide, and $\mathbf v_G$ points up and to the right at 21.2° above the horizontal.

Check: $\mathbf v_G$ must equal the average of $\mathbf v_A$ and $\mathbf v_B$, because G is the midpoint. $\tfrac12\left[(1.585 + 2.5)\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 1.585\,\hat{\mathbf j}\right] = 2.04\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.79\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ m/s, whose magnitude is 2.19 m/s.

Answer

(a) $\omega_{AB} = 3.05$ rad/s clockwise. (b) $v_A = 2.24$ m/s, up the guide. (c) $v_G = 2.19$ m/s.
