Example 1.A.1Pin in a fixed ring and a moving slot

Cartesianconstraintchain rule

Problem

Pin P is constrained to move along a fixed elliptical ring whose shape is given by $x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1$, where $x$ and $y$ are in millimetres. The pin is also constrained to move within a horizontal slot that moves upward at a constant speed $v$. The pin is on the left half of the ring, as in the figure.

The data are $a = 5$ mm, $b = 10$ mm and $v = 30$ mm/s.

Find (a) the velocity and (b) the acceleration of pin P at the position where $y = 6$ mm.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose a description and draw the kinematics diagram

The slot sets the vertical position of the pin, and the ring relates $x$ to $y$. Both constraints are written most simply in Cartesian coordinates, so we use them. The slot moves upward at constant speed, so $\dot y = v = 30$ mm/s and $\ddot y = 0$.

The slot gives $y$ and $\dot y$ directly. The ring then fixes $x$, $\dot x$ and $\ddot x$. The acceleration of P is horizontal because the slot does not accelerate.

Step 2 · Find the position

At $y = 6$ mm the ring equation gives

$$x = -a\sqrt{1 - \frac{y^2}{b^2}} = -5\sqrt{1 - 0.36} = -4\ \text{mm}$$

The negative root is taken because the pin is on the left half of the ring.

Step 3 · Differentiate the ring equation once to find the velocity

The pin stays on the ring at every instant, so the time derivative of the ring equation is also zero.

$$\frac{2x\dot x}{a^2} + \frac{2y\dot y}{b^2} = 0 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \dot x = -\frac{a^2}{b^2}\,\frac{y\,\dot y}{x} = -\frac{25}{100}\cdot\frac{(6)(30)}{-4} = 11.25\ \text{mm/s}$$
Step 4 · Differentiate again to find the acceleration

Differentiating $x\dot x/a^2 + y\dot y/b^2 = 0$ once more with the product rule gives

$$\frac{\dot x^2 + x\ddot x}{a^2} + \frac{\dot y^2 + y\ddot y}{b^2} = 0 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \ddot x = -\frac{1}{x}\left(\dot x^2 + \frac{a^2}{b^2}\left(\dot y^2 + y\ddot y\right)\right)$$

With $\ddot y = 0$,

$$\ddot x = -\frac{1}{-4}\left(11.25^2 + 0.25\,(30)^2\right) = \frac{126.6 + 225}{4} = 87.89\ \text{mm/s}^2$$
Answer

(a) $\mathbf v_P = 11.25\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 30\,\hat{\mathbf j}$ mm/s, with magnitude 32.0 mm/s.

(b) $\mathbf a_P = 87.89\,\hat{\mathbf i}$ mm/s². The acceleration is horizontal because the slot does not accelerate.

Explore

  • Change the slot speed $v$. Both $\dot x$ and $\ddot x$ change, but $\ddot x$ grows with $v^2$. Explain why from the equations.
  • Watch $\dot x$ as $y$ approaches $b$. Why does it grow without bound at the top of the ring?
  • Put the pin on the right half of the ring instead. Which signs in the answer change?
