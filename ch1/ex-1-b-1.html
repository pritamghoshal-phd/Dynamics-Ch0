Example 1.B.1Mixing device

cylindrical3Dmaximum

Problem

In the mixing device shown, a disk turns about a vertical shaft at a constant rate $\dot\theta = \omega$. At the same time the shaft moves up and down with $z = z_0\sin 2\pi n t$, where $n$ is a constant. Point A is on the rim of the disk at radius $r$.

Find the maximum magnitude of the acceleration of point A.

The animation uses the sample values $r = 0.1$ m, $\omega = 10$ rad/s, $z_0 = 0.05$ m and $n = 2$ Hz, which you can change.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose a description and draw the kinematics diagram

Point A turns about a fixed vertical axis and moves along that axis, so cylindrical coordinates fit the motion. For A the radius $r$ is constant, $\dot\theta = \omega$ is constant, and $z = z_0\sin 2\pi n t$.

$$\mathbf a = \left(\ddot r - r\dot\theta^2\right)\hat{\mathbf e}_r + \left(r\ddot\theta + 2\dot r\dot\theta\right)\hat{\mathbf e}_\theta + \ddot z\,\hat{\mathbf k}$$

Point A has a constant radius and a constant spin rate, so its acceleration has only the centripetal part $r\omega^2$ (toward the shaft) and the vertical part $\ddot z$ of the stroke.

Step 2 · Find the time derivatives $$\dot r = \ddot r = 0, \qquad \ddot\theta = 0, \qquad \dot z = 2\pi n z_0\cos 2\pi n t, \qquad \ddot z = -(2\pi n)^2 z_0\sin 2\pi n t$$
Step 3 · Write the acceleration $$\mathbf a = -r\omega^2\,\hat{\mathbf e}_r - (2\pi n)^2 z_0\sin(2\pi n t)\,\hat{\mathbf k}$$

The radial part is the centripetal acceleration of the spin, and the vertical part comes from the stroke of the shaft. There is no transverse part, because both $\dot r$ and $\ddot\theta$ are zero.

Step 4 · Maximize the magnitude $$|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{r^2\omega^4 + (2\pi n)^4 z_0^2\sin^2 2\pi n t}$$

The first term under the root is constant. The second is largest when $\sin^2 2\pi n t = 1$, which happens at the top and at the bottom of the stroke.

Answer

$\displaystyle |\mathbf a|_{\max} = \sqrt{r^2\omega^4 + (2\pi n)^4 z_0^2}$

With the sample values, $|\mathbf a|_{\max} = \sqrt{10^2 + 7.896^2} = 12.74$ m/s².

Explore

  • Set $z_0 = 0$. What is the acceleration of A now, and in which direction does it point?
  • Double $n$. The vertical part of the maximum grows by a factor of 4. Find this factor from the formula.
  • At which height of the disk is the magnitude of the acceleration of A largest? Check your answer with the graph.
