Example 1.C.2Pin driven by two slotted guides

Cartesianpath componentsradius of curvature

Problem

Pin P is constrained to move in two slotted guides that move at right angles to one another. Guide A, whose slot is vertical, moves to the right with a speed $v_A$ that changes at the rate $\dot v_A$. At the same time, guide B, whose slot is horizontal, moves downward with a speed $v_B$ that changes at the rate $\dot v_B$.

The data are $v_A = 0.2$ m/s, $v_B = 0.15$ m/s, $\dot v_A = 0.75$ m/s² and $\dot v_B = 0$.

Find (a) the rate of change of speed of P at this instant and (b) the radius of curvature $\rho$ of the path followed by P at this instant.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Relate the motion of P to the guides

The vertical slot of guide A forces P to have the same horizontal position as guide A, and the horizontal slot of guide B forces P to have the same vertical position as guide B. With $\hat{\mathbf i}$ to the right and $\hat{\mathbf j}$ upward, the Cartesian velocity and acceleration of P are therefore

$$\mathbf v_P = v_A\,\hat{\mathbf i} - v_B\,\hat{\mathbf j} = 0.2\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.15\,\hat{\mathbf j}\ \text{m/s}$$ $$\mathbf a_P = \dot v_A\,\hat{\mathbf i} - \dot v_B\,\hat{\mathbf j} = 0.75\,\hat{\mathbf i}\ \text{m/s}^2$$
Step 2 · Draw the kinematics diagram

The pin moves with guide A horizontally and with guide B vertically, so its velocity has components $v_A$ and $v_B$. Only guide A speeds up, so the acceleration of P points to the right.

Step 3 · Find the path unit vectors

The speed of P is $v = \sqrt{0.2^2 + 0.15^2} = 0.25$ m/s. The tangent unit vector points along the velocity.

$$\hat{\mathbf e}_t = \frac{\mathbf v_P}{v} = 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf i} - 0.6\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$

The normal unit vector is perpendicular to $\hat{\mathbf e}_t$, on the side of the path toward which the acceleration points. The acceleration points to the right, which is on the upper side of the velocity, so

$$\hat{\mathbf e}_n = 0.6\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf j}$$
Step 4 · Project the acceleration onto the path directions $$a_t = \mathbf a_P\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}_t = 0.75(0.8) = 0.60\ \text{m/s}^2, \qquad a_n = \mathbf a_P\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}_n = 0.75(0.6) = 0.45\ \text{m/s}^2$$

As a check, $\sqrt{a_t^2 + a_n^2} = \sqrt{0.36 + 0.2025} = 0.75$ m/s², which is the magnitude of $\mathbf a_P$.

Step 5 · Find the rate of change of speed and the radius of curvature $$\dot v = a_t = 0.60\ \text{m/s}^2$$ $$\rho = \frac{v^2}{a_n} = \frac{(0.25)^2}{0.45} = 0.139\ \text{m}$$

The same results follow without unit vectors from $\dot v = \mathbf v\cdot\mathbf a / v$ and $a_n = |\mathbf v\times\mathbf a|/v$.

Answer

(a) $\dot v = 0.600$ m/s², so P is speeding up. (b) $\rho = 0.139$ m.
