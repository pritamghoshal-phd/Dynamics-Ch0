Example 1.D.3Block pulled by a cable wound between two blocks

constrained motioncables and pulleysdifferentiation

Problem

Block B has a constant acceleration of $a_B = 3$ m/s² to the right. At the instant shown, B has a velocity of 2 m/s to the right. A single inextensible cable is attached to B, wraps around a pulley on A, a pulley on B and a second pulley on A, and ends at a post fixed to the floor between the blocks. The cable segments are horizontal.

Find (a) the velocity of block A and (b) the acceleration of block A.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose coordinates from a fixed line

Measure the positions $x_A$ and $x_B$ of the two blocks to the right from the same fixed line. Every length we write must be a difference between positions measured from fixed points, so that the constant parts drop out when we differentiate.

Step 2 · Draw the cable diagram

Measure $x_A$ and $x_B$ from the same fixed line to the pulleys on A and B. Segments 1, 2 and 3 each have length $x_B - x_A$ plus a constant, and segment 4 has length $x_{\text{post}} - x_A$ plus a constant.

Step 3 · Write the length of the cable

Segments 1, 2 and 3 run between a point on B and a pulley on A, so each has length $x_B - x_A$ plus a constant. Segment 4 runs from a pulley on A to the fixed post, so its length is $x_{\text{post}} - x_A$ plus a constant. The parts wrapped around the pulleys have constant lengths. Therefore

$$L = 3\left(x_B - x_A\right) + \left(x_{\text{post}} - x_A\right) + \text{constant} = 3x_B - 4x_A + \text{constant}$$
Step 4 · Differentiate

The cable is inextensible, so $\dot L = 0$ and $\ddot L = 0$.

$$0 = 3\dot x_B - 4\dot x_A \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dot x_A = \tfrac34\,\dot x_B, \qquad \ddot x_A = \tfrac34\,\ddot x_B$$
Step 5 · Solve $$v_A = \tfrac34(2) = 1.5\ \text{m/s}, \qquad a_A = \tfrac34(3) = 2.25\ \text{m/s}^2$$

Both results are positive, so A moves and accelerates to the right, in the same direction as B. As a check, the cable speed in segment 4 is $4v_A - 3v_B = 0$, as it must be for a segment that ends at a fixed post.

Answer

(a) $\mathbf v_A = 1.5$ m/s to the right. (b) $\mathbf a_A = 2.25$ m/s² to the right.
