Example 1.D.5Two cables and two movable pulleys

constrained motiontwo cablespulleys

Problem

The system contains two inextensible cables. Cable 1 runs from B over a fixed pulley, down and under the pulley that carries block $m$, and up to the axle of pulley C. Cable 2 runs from A over a second fixed pulley, down and under pulley C, and up to a fixed bracket. End A moves to the right with a speed of $v_A = 2$ m/s, and end B moves to the left with a speed of $v_B = 2$ m/s.

Find the speed of block $m$.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose coordinates

Measure $x_A$ to the right and $x_B$ to the left from the fixed pulleys. Measure $x_C$ and $x_m$ downward from the ceiling to the centres of pulley C and of the pulley that carries $m$. With these choices, $\dot x_A = 2$ m/s and $\dot x_B = 2$ m/s.

Step 2 · Draw the cable diagram

Cable 1 (orange) runs from B over the left pulley, under the pulley of $m$, and up to the axle of C. Cable 2 (blue) runs from A over the right pulley, under C, and up to the bracket of that pulley. All coordinates are measured from fixed points.

Step 3 · Write the length of each cable

Cable 2 consists of the horizontal segment to A and two vertical strands that hold C.

$$L_2 = x_A + 2x_C + \text{constant}$$

Cable 1 consists of the horizontal segment to B, the strand from the left fixed pulley down to the pulley of $m$, and the strand from the pulley of $m$ up to C. The last strand has length $x_m - x_C$.

$$L_1 = x_B + x_m + \left(x_m - x_C\right) + \text{constant} = x_B + 2x_m - x_C + \text{constant}$$
Step 4 · Differentiate $$0 = \dot x_A + 2\dot x_C \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dot x_C = -\tfrac12\,\dot x_A = -1\ \text{m/s}$$ $$0 = \dot x_B + 2\dot x_m - \dot x_C \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \dot x_m = \tfrac12\left(\dot x_C - \dot x_B\right)$$
Step 5 · Solve $$\dot x_m = \tfrac12\left(-1 - 2\right) = -1.5\ \text{m/s}$$

The negative sign means that $x_m$ decreases, so block $m$ moves upward. Both effects lift $m$. Pulling B shortens cable 1 directly, and pulling A raises C, which takes more of cable 1 into the strand between C and $m$.

Answer

Block $m$ moves upward with a speed of 1.5 m/s.
