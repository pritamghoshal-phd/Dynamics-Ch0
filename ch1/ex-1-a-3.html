Example 1.A.3Radius of curvature of a jet’s path

path componentsradius of curvaturenormal acceleration

Problem

A jet is flying on the curved path shown with a speed $v$. At position A on the loop, the speed of the jet is $v = 600$ km/h, the magnitude of its acceleration is $2.5g$ and the tangential component of its acceleration is $a_t = 5$ m/s².

Find the radius of curvature of the path of the jet at A.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Choose a description and draw the kinematics diagram

The data are the speed and the tangential acceleration, and we want the radius of curvature. These quantities belong to the path (normal–tangential) description,

$$\mathbf a = \dot v\,\hat{\mathbf e}_t + \frac{v^2}{\rho}\,\hat{\mathbf e}_n = a_t\,\hat{\mathbf e}_t + a_n\,\hat{\mathbf e}_n$$

The unit vector $\hat{\mathbf e}_t$ is tangent to the path, and $\hat{\mathbf e}_n$ points toward the center of curvature C.

The tangential component $a_t$ changes the speed and the normal component $a_n = v^2/\rho$ turns the velocity toward the center of curvature C. The total acceleration has magnitude 2.5g.

Step 2 · Find the normal component of the acceleration

The two components are perpendicular, so the magnitude of the acceleration is $|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{a_t^2 + a_n^2}$. With $|\mathbf a| = 2.5(9.81) = 24.53$ m/s²,

$$a_n = \sqrt{|\mathbf a|^2 - a_t^2} = \sqrt{24.53^2 - 5^2} = 24.01\ \text{m/s}^2$$
Step 3 · Find the radius of curvature

Convert the speed to metres per second, $v = 600/3.6 = 166.7$ m/s. The normal component is $a_n = v^2/\rho$, so

$$\rho = \frac{v^2}{a_n} = \frac{166.7^2}{24.01} = 1157\ \text{m}$$
Answer

$\rho = 1157$ m, or about 1.16 km.

Explore

  • Set $a_t = 0$. How much does the radius of curvature change, and why is the change so small?
  • Increase the acceleration limit to 6g. How tight can the turn be at 600 km/h?
  • Follow the jet past A. The curvature of the path keeps growing. What happens to the normal acceleration?
