Example 4.A.5A cable car pulled up a taut cable

Newton's lawnormal forceinclined path

Problem

The cable car C, of mass $m = 200$ kg, rolls on a taut straight cable that rises with a slope of 5 vertical to 12 horizontal. A horizontal force $F = 2400$ N pulls the car up the cable.

Find (a) the acceleration of the cable car and (b) the total force that the cable car exerts on the cable.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method: free-body diagrams, kinetics equations, kinematics, solve. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

Three forces act on the car. The weight $mg$ acts downward, and the horizontal force $F$ acts to the right. The wheels roll freely on the cable, so the cable can push on the car only perpendicular to itself, with the normal force $N$. We take $x$ up the cable and $y$ perpendicular to it. The cable makes the angle $\beta$ with the horizontal, with $\sin\beta = 5/13$ and $\cos\beta = 12/13$.

The wheels roll freely on the cable, so the cable can push on the car only perpendicular to itself, with the normal force $N$. The $x$ axis points up the cable, at $\beta = 22.62°$ above the horizontal ($\tan\beta = 5/12$).

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equations $$\sum F_x = F\cos\beta - mg\sin\beta = m a, \qquad \sum F_y = N - F\sin\beta - mg\cos\beta = 0$$

The component of $F$ perpendicular to the cable points into the cable, so it adds to the weight component.

Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

The car moves along the straight cable, so its acceleration points along the cable, $\mathbf a = a\,\hat{\imath}$.

Step 4 · Solve $$a = \frac{F\cos\beta}{m} - g\sin\beta = \frac{2400(12/13)}{200} - 9.81\left(\frac{5}{13}\right) = 11.08 - 3.77 = 7.30\ \text{m/s}^2$$ $$N = F\sin\beta + mg\cos\beta = 2400\left(\frac{5}{13}\right) + 200(9.81)\left(\frac{12}{13}\right) = 923.1 + 1811.1 = 2734\ \text{N}$$

By Newton's third law the car pushes on the cable with a force of the same size in the opposite direction, perpendicular to the cable and toward the lower right.

Answer

(a) $a = 7.30$ m/s² up the cable. (b) The car pushes on the cable with 2734 N, perpendicular to the cable.
