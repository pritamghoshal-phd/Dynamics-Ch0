Example 4.C.11Oblique impact of two spheres

oblique impactrestitutionline of impact

Problem

Sphere A has a mass of 20 kg and a radius of 75 mm. Sphere B has a mass of 5 kg and a radius of 48 mm. The spheres move in a horizontal plane on parallel lines, A to the right at $v_{A1} = 5$ m/s and B to the left at $v_{B1} = 15$ m/s. The line along which the center of B moves is tangent to the bottom of A, as the animation shows, so the spheres strike each other off-center. The coefficient of restitution is $e = 0.6$.

Find the velocities of the spheres immediately after the impact.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method: free-body diagrams, kinetics equations, kinematics, solve. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

We draw each sphere seen from above. The only impulsive force on each one is the contact force $F$, which acts along the line of impact $n$ through the two centers. Its size is the same on both spheres and its directions are opposite. The weights and the normal forces from the plane are vertical and do not enter the in-plane equations.

Seen from above, the only impulsive force on each sphere is the contact force $F$. It acts along the line of impact $n$, which joins the two centers at the moment of contact, with equal size and opposite directions on A and B. The weights and the normal forces from the table are perpendicular to the plane, and friction is neglected, so nothing acts along $t$.

Step 2 · Find the line of impact

At contact the centers are $r_A + r_B = 123$ mm apart. The paths of the centers are $r_A = 75$ mm apart, so the line joining the centers makes the angle $\phi$ with the direction of motion, where

$$\sin\phi = \frac{75}{123}, \qquad \phi = 37.6°$$

With $x$ to the right and $y$ up in the top view, B is below and to the right of A. The unit vectors along the line of impact and the tangent are

$$\hat n = \cos\phi\,\hat\imath - \sin\phi\,\hat\jmath = 0.7926\,\hat\imath - 0.6098\,\hat\jmath, \qquad \hat t = \sin\phi\,\hat\imath + \cos\phi\,\hat\jmath$$
Step 3 · Write the kinetics equations

Resolve the velocities before the impact into $n$ and $t$ components.

$$v_{A1n} = 5\cos\phi = 3.963, \quad v_{A1t} = 5\sin\phi = 3.049, \quad v_{B1n} = -15\cos\phi = -11.889, \quad v_{B1t} = -15\sin\phi = -9.146\ \text{m/s}$$

No impulse acts along $t$ on either sphere, so the $t$ component of each velocity is unchanged. Along $n$, momentum of the pair is conserved and the coefficient of restitution applies.

$$m_A v_{A1n} + m_B v_{B1n} = m_A v_{A2n} + m_B v_{B2n}$$ $$v_{B2n} - v_{A2n} = -e\left(v_{B1n} - v_{A1n}\right)$$
Step 4 · Solve

The momentum equation gives $20\,v_{A2n} + 5\,v_{B2n} = 19.81$ and the restitution equation gives $v_{B2n} - v_{A2n} = 9.511$. Solving these two equations together,

$$v_{A2n} = -1.110\ \text{m/s}, \qquad v_{B2n} = 8.401\ \text{m/s}$$

Combining the new $n$ components with the unchanged $t$ components,

$$\mathbf v_{A2} = -1.110\,\hat n + 3.049\,\hat t = 0.980\,\hat\imath + 3.093\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$ $$\mathbf v_{B2} = 8.401\,\hat n - 9.146\,\hat t = 1.082\,\hat\imath - 12.372\,\hat\jmath\ \text{m/s}$$

A leaves at 3.24 m/s, 72.4° above the $x$ axis, and B leaves at 12.42 m/s, 85.0° below the $x$ axis. The kinetic energy drops from 812.5 J to 490.9 J.

Answer

$\mathbf v_{A2} = 0.980\,\hat\imath + 3.09\,\hat\jmath$ m/s (3.24 m/s) and $\mathbf v_{B2} = 1.08\,\hat\imath - 12.37\,\hat\jmath$ m/s (12.42 m/s), with $x$ along the original motion of A and $y$ toward A's side of the paths.

Explore

  • Set $e = 0$. The $n$ components become equal after the impact. Do the spheres move together afterward? Why not?
  • Set $v_{B1} = 0$. In which direction does B leave, and why is that direction the line of impact?
