Example 4.C.12Cue ball sending the object ball into the side pocket

oblique impactbilliardsrestitution

Problem

Cue ball A strikes the stationary object ball B with speed $v_{A1}$, moving at the angle $\theta_1$ to the cushion. After the impact, B moves straight toward the side pocket, perpendicular to the cushion, and A moves along a line at the angle $\theta_2$ to the cushion. The balls have equal masses, and the coefficient of restitution is $e$.

The data are $\theta_1 = 45°$ and $e = 0.9$.

Find the rebound angle $\theta_2$ of A.

Solution

Every solution follows the four-step method: free-body diagrams, kinetics equations, kinematics, solve. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

Seen from above, each ball receives one impulsive force at the impact, the contact force $F$ along the line of impact. Because B leaves straight toward the pocket, the impulse on B points toward the pocket. The line of impact $n$ is therefore perpendicular to the cushion, and the tangent direction $t$ is parallel to it.

B moves straight toward the pocket, so the impulse on B, and therefore the line of impact $n$, points from the center of A to the center of B. The impulse on A has the same size and points the other way. Along $t$ there is no impulse on either ball.

Step 2 · Write the kinetics equations

Before the impact, A has the components $v_{A1t} = v_{A1}\cos\theta_1$ and $v_{A1n} = v_{A1}\sin\theta_1$. B is at rest. Along $t$ there is no impulse on either ball, so

$$v_{A2t} = v_{A1}\cos\theta_1, \qquad v_{B2t} = 0$$

Along $n$, momentum is conserved and the coefficient of restitution applies. With equal masses,

$$v_{A1n} = v_{A2n} + v_{B2n}, \qquad v_{B2n} - v_{A2n} = e\,v_{A1n}$$
Step 3 · Write the kinematics equations

The rebound angle of A is measured from the cushion, which is the $t$ direction, so

$$\tan\theta_2 = \frac{v_{A2n}}{v_{A2t}}$$
Step 4 · Solve

Subtracting the restitution equation from the momentum equation gives $2\,v_{A2n} = (1 - e)\,v_{A1n}$. Therefore

$$\tan\theta_2 = \frac{(1 - e)\,v_{A1}\sin\theta_1/2}{v_{A1}\cos\theta_1} = \frac{1 - e}{2}\,\tan\theta_1 = \frac{0.1}{2}\,(1) = 0.05$$ $$\theta_2 = 2.86°$$

The result does not depend on the speed of the cue ball. With a perfectly elastic impact ($e = 1$), A would leave exactly along the cushion, at 90° to the path of B, which is the well-known rule for equal billiard balls.

Answer

$\theta_2 = 2.86°$ from the cushion.

Explore

  • Set $e = 1$. Check that the two balls leave at a right angle to each other.
  • Change $\theta_1$ to 60°. Show with the formula that $\theta_2$ becomes 4.95°, and compare with the animation.
