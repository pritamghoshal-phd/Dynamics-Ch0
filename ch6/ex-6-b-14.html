Example 6.B.14Critical damping of a pinned bar

rotational EOMcritical dampingsmall angles

Problem

A homogeneous bar of mass $m$ and length $b$ is pinned to ground at point O and moves in a horizontal plane. A spring of stiffness $k$ is attached at a distance $a$ from O, and a dashpot with constant $c$ is attached at the end of the bar, a distance $b$ from O. The other ends of the spring and the dashpot are fixed to ground. Let $\theta$ be the angular orientation of the bar, measured counterclockwise from the position where the spring is unstretched.

Find the value of $c$ for critical damping. Assume small oscillations, so that $\sin\theta \approx \theta$.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

Weight and the vertical pin force are perpendicular to the plane of motion, so they have no moment about the vertical axis through O.

For a small counterclockwise rotation $\theta$, the spring end moves sideways by $a\theta$ and the dashpot end by $b\theta$ with speed $b\dot\theta$. Both forces push the bar back, perpendicular to it.

Step 2 · Take moments about the fixed pin O

The pin force passes through O. The bar turns about the fixed point O, so

$$\sum M_O = I_O\ddot\theta: \qquad -(k\,a\theta)\,a - (c\,b\dot\theta)\,b = \tfrac13 m b^2\,\ddot\theta$$

Here $I_O = \tfrac13 m b^2$ is the moment of inertia of a uniform bar about its end.

Step 3 · Write the equation of motion in standard form $$\tfrac13 m b^2\,\ddot\theta + c\,b^2\,\dot\theta + k\,a^2\,\theta = 0$$

This has the form $m_{eq}\ddot\theta + c_{eq}\dot\theta + k_{eq}\theta = 0$ with $m_{eq} = \tfrac13 mb^2$, $c_{eq} = cb^2$ and $k_{eq} = ka^2$. So

$$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k a^2}{\tfrac13 m b^2}} = \frac{a}{b}\sqrt{\frac{3k}{m}}, \qquad \zeta = \frac{c_{eq}}{2\sqrt{k_{eq}m_{eq}}} = \frac{c\,b^2}{2\sqrt{k a^2\cdot\tfrac13 m b^2}}$$
Step 4 · Impose critical damping

Critical damping means $\zeta = 1$:

$$c\,b^2 = 2\sqrt{k a^2\cdot\tfrac13 m b^2} = 2ab\sqrt{\frac{km}{3}} \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad c_{cr} = \frac{2a}{b}\sqrt{\frac{km}{3}}$$

With the animation values $m = 3$ kg, $b = 1.2$ m, $a = 0.7$ m and $k = 200$ N/m this gives $c_{cr} = 16.5$ N·s/m.

Answer $$c_{cr} = \frac{2a}{b}\sqrt{\frac{km}{3}}$$
