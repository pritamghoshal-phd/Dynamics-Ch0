Example 6.C.10Mass hung from a pulley and driven through a cable

base excitationpulleysresonance

Problem

The base B moves horizontally with $x_B(t) = b\cos\omega t$. A spring of stiffness $k$ connects B to the end E of a cable. The cable passes over a fixed pulley, runs down and around a movable pulley, and is tied to the yoke of the fixed pulley. A block of mass $m$ hangs from the movable pulley, and a second spring of stiffness $k$ connects the block to the ground. The pulleys are light and smooth, and the coordinate $x$ of the block is measured downward from static equilibrium.

Find (a) the differential equation of motion for the system and (b) the resonant frequency of the system.

The book gives no numbers. The animation uses $m = 5$ kg, $k = 200$ N/m and $b = 20$ mm.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

Take the movable pulley and the block together. Two strands of the cable pull up on the pulley with the tension $T$. The lower spring pushes or pulls on the block with $F_2$, and the weight acts down. The cable end E is massless, so the upper spring force $F_1$ equals $T$.

Left: two strands of the cable hold up the movable pulley, so the cable tension $T$ appears twice. $F_2$ is the force of the lower spring. Right: the end E of the upper spring is massless, so the spring force $F_1$ equals the cable tension. The pulleys are light and smooth, so $T$ is the same in every strand.

Step 2 · Write the kinematics of the cable

When the block moves down by $x$, both strands that hold the movable pulley become longer by $x$. The cable has a fixed length, so the end E moves toward the fixed pulley by $2x$. The upper spring is then stretched by $2x - x_B$ beyond its static stretch.

$$T = F_1 = T_{st} + k\,(2x - x_B), \qquad F_2 = F_{2,st} + kx$$

Here $F_2$ acts up on the block when $x > 0$, because the lower spring is compressed further.

Step 3 · Write the equation of motion

Newton's second law for the pulley and the block, with $x$ downward, gives

$$m\ddot x = mg - 2T - F_2 = mg - 2T_{st} - F_{2,st} - 2k\,(2x - x_B) - kx$$

The static terms balance the weight, $2T_{st} + F_{2,st} = mg$. What remains is

$$m\ddot x + 5kx = 2k\,x_B = 2k\,b\cos\omega t$$
Step 4 · Find the resonant frequency

Resonance occurs when the forcing frequency equals the natural frequency of the equation of motion.

$$\omega_{res} = \omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{5k}{m}}$$

The steady-state amplitude is $X = 2kb/(5k - m\omega^2)$, which grows without bound as $\omega$ approaches $\sqrt{5k/m}$. With the animation's values, $\omega_n = \sqrt{1000/5} = 14.14$ rad/s.

Answer

(a) $m\ddot x + 5kx = 2k\,b\cos\omega t$. (b) The resonant frequency is $\omega = \sqrt{5k/m}$.
