Example 6.C.6Trailer on a wavy road

base excitationresonancecritical speed

Problem

A trailer of mass $m = 400$ kg is towed at speed $v = 25$ km/h along a road whose surface is a sine wave with an amplitude of $50$ mm and a wavelength of $1.2$ m. Adding $75$ kg of mass to the trailer makes its suspension sag an additional $3$ mm. Treat the trailer as a mass on a single spring and neglect damping.

Find (a) the vibration amplitude $X$ of the trailer, and (b) the critical speed at which the undamped oscillations are largest.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Find the spring stiffness

The extra 75 kg compresses the suspension by 3 mm, so

$$k = \frac{75(9.81)}{0.003} = 2.453 \times 10^5\ \text{N/m}, \qquad \omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{245\,250}{400}} = 24.76\ \text{rad/s}$$
Step 2 · Draw the free-body diagram

We isolate the trailer body and measure $y$ from its static equilibrium position. The wheel follows the road, so the axle has a prescribed vertical motion $y_B(t)$.

The spring force in the static position balances the weight. During the motion the spring is shortened by the extra amount $y_B - y$, where $y_B$ is the vertical position of the wheel axle and $y$ that of the body.

Newton's second law gives $m\ddot y = mg + k(y_B - y) - mg$, so

$$m\ddot y + ky = ky_B(t)$$
Step 3 · Find the excitation frequency

The wheel meets one full wave every time it travels one wavelength $\lambda = 1.2$ m, so the base motion is $y_B = b\sin\omega t$ with $b = 0.05$ m and

$$\omega = \frac{2\pi v}{\lambda} = \frac{2\pi(25/3.6)}{1.2} = 36.36\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad r = \frac{\omega}{\omega_n} = \frac{36.36}{24.76} = 1.468$$
Step 4 · Find the amplitude $$X = \frac{b}{\left|1 - r^2\right|} = \frac{50}{\left|1 - 1.468^2\right|} = \frac{50}{1.157} = 43.2\ \text{mm}$$

Since $r > 1$, the trailer body moves opposite to the axle. It is up while the wheel is in a trough and down while the wheel is on a crest.

Step 5 · Find the critical speed

The undamped response is largest at resonance, $\omega = \omega_n$. The corresponding speed is

$$v_c = \frac{\omega_n\lambda}{2\pi} = \frac{24.76(1.2)}{2\pi} = 4.73\ \text{m/s} = 17.0\ \text{km/h}$$

The animation uses a wheel of radius 90 mm. Because a round wheel cannot reach the bottom of a narrow trough in exactly the same way as a point follower, its axle moves almost, but not exactly, like a sine wave. The simulated steady-state amplitude is therefore within a few percent of the 43.2 mm found with the ideal sine input.

Answer

(a) $X = 43.2$ mm at 25 km/h, with the body moving opposite to the axle.

(b) The critical speed is $v_c = 17.0$ km/h.
