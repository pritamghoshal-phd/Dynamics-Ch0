Chapter 6 · Vibrations

Many machines and structures move back and forth about an equilibrium position. This chapter shows how to derive the equation of motion of such a system from free-body diagrams, how the natural frequency and the damping ratio control its free response, and how it responds when it is driven by a harmonic force or by a moving support. Every simulation solves the equation of motion live, so each slider changes a real model.

Every slider has a box next to it. You can type any number into the box and press Enter, including values beyond the slider range, zero or negative numbers, and the page explains what happens physically.
