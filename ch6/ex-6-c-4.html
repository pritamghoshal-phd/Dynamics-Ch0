Example 6.C.4Rolling disk driven through a spring by a moving base

base excitationrolling without slippingphase

Problem

A homogeneous disk of mass $m$ and radius $R$ rolls without slipping. Its center O is connected to the wall by a spring of stiffness $k$ and to a massless moving base B by a spring of stiffness $4k$. The base is given the motion $x_B(t) = b\sin\omega t$. The coordinate $x$ gives the position of O, and both springs are unstretched when $x = x_B = 0$.

Find (a) the equation of motion of the disk in terms of $x$, (b) the particular solution of the equation of motion, and (c) the amplitude of the response for $\omega = 15$ rad/s. Is the response in phase or out of phase with the forcing?

Use $m = 4$ kg, $k = 800$ N/m and $b = 20$ mm.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

We isolate the disk at a position $x$ while the base is at $x_B$.

The left spring is stretched by $x$ and pulls O to the left. The right spring is stretched by $x_B - x$ and pulls O to the right. Writing both spring forces this way keeps the signs right for any $x$ and $x_B$.

Step 2 · Write the Newton–Euler equations and the rolling condition

As in Example 6.C.1, the moment equation about O together with $\ddot x = R\alpha$ gives the friction force $f = -\tfrac12 m\ddot x$. The force equation then becomes

$$-kx + 4k(x_B - x) + f = m\ddot x \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \tfrac32 m\ddot x + 5kx = 4kb\sin\omega t$$

The moving base acts like a harmonic force of amplitude $4kb$. The natural frequency is

$$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{5k}{\tfrac32 m}} = \sqrt{\frac{10k}{3m}} = \sqrt{\frac{10(800)}{3(4)}} = 25.82\ \text{rad/s}$$
Step 3 · Find the particular solution

With $x_p = X\sin\omega t$,

$$\left(5k - \tfrac32 m\omega^2\right)X = 4kb \quad\Longrightarrow\quad X = \frac{(4/5)\,b}{1 - r^2}, \qquad r = \frac{\omega}{\omega_n}$$ $$x_p(t) = \frac{0.8\,b}{1 - (\omega/\omega_n)^2}\,\sin\omega t$$
Step 4 · Evaluate at ω = 15 rad/s

The frequency ratio is $r = 15/25.82 = 0.581$, so $r^2 = 0.3375$.

$$X = \frac{0.8(20)}{1 - 0.3375} = \frac{16}{0.6625} = 24.2\ \text{mm}$$

$X$ is positive, so the disk moves in phase with the base motion, as expected below resonance.

Answer

(a) $\tfrac32 m\ddot x + 5kx = 4kb\sin\omega t$, with $\omega_n = \sqrt{10k/3m} = 25.8$ rad/s.

(b) $x_p(t) = \dfrac{0.8\,b}{1 - (\omega/\omega_n)^2}\sin\omega t$.

(c) $X = 24.2$ mm at $\omega = 15$ rad/s, in phase with the base motion.
