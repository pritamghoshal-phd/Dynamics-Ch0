Example 6.C.9Machine on four springs on a vibrating floor

base excitationisolationparallel springs

Problem

A mass $m$ rests on four identical springs of stiffness $k$, which stand on a base B. The base moves vertically with $x_B(t) = 0.002\sin 50t$ m. The coordinate $x$ of the mass is measured upward from its static equilibrium position.

The data are $m = 50$ kg and $k = 7500$ N/m.

Find (a) the differential equation of motion for the system and (b) the amplitude of the steady-state response.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

Draw the mass displaced upward by $x$ while the base is displaced by $x_B$. Each spring is compressed by $\delta_{st} + x_B - x$, where $\delta_{st}$ is the static compression, and pushes up on the mass with $k(\delta_{st} + x_B - x)$.

Each of the four springs pushes up on the mass with $F_s = k(\delta_{st} + x_B - x)$, where $\delta_{st}$ is the static compression. The weight acts down. With $x$ measured from static equilibrium, $4k\delta_{st} = mg$ cancels the weight.

Step 2 · Write the equation of motion

Newton's second law in the vertical direction gives

$$m\ddot x = 4k\left(\delta_{st} + x_B - x\right) - mg$$

In static equilibrium $4k\,\delta_{st} = mg$, so these two terms cancel.

$$m\ddot x + 4kx = 4k\,x_B = 4k\,(0.002)\sin 50t$$
Step 3 · Find the natural frequency and the frequency ratio $$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{4k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{4(7500)}{50}} = 24.49\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad r = \frac{\omega}{\omega_n} = \frac{50}{24.49} = 2.041$$
Step 4 · Find the steady-state amplitude

The particular solution is $x_p = X\sin 50t$ with

$$X = \frac{b}{1 - r^2} = \frac{0.002}{1 - 4.167} = -0.000632\ \text{m}$$

The amplitude is $|X| = 0.632$ mm. The negative sign means that the mass moves opposite to the base. Because $r > \sqrt2$, the springs isolate the mass: it moves less than the base does.

Answer

(a) $m\ddot x + 4kx = 4k\,x_B(t)$, which is $50\,\ddot x + 30\,000\,x = 60\sin 50t$ (N). (b) The steady-state amplitude is $|X| = 0.632$ mm, and the mass moves opposite to the base.

  • Lower $\omega$ until the mass moves more than the base. At what frequency does $|X| = b$?
