Example 6.C.5Block in a shaken box

base excitationfrequency responsephase

Problem

Block A, of mass $m$, rests on the smooth floor of box B and is connected to both end walls of the box by springs of stiffness $k$. Box B is given the prescribed displacement $x_B(t) = b\sin\omega t$. The coordinate $x$ gives the absolute position of A, and both springs are unstretched when $x = x_B = 0$.

Find the two values of the frequency $\omega$ for which the amplitude of the forced response of block A is twice that of box B.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

We isolate block A at position $x$ while the box is at $x_B$.

When A moves a distance $x - x_B$ to the right relative to the box, the left spring is stretched and pulls A back, and the right spring is compressed and pushes A back. Both forces equal $k(x - x_B)$ and point to the left. The floor of the box is smooth.

Step 2 · Write the equation of motion

Newton's second law in the $x$ direction gives

$$-2k(x - x_B) = m\ddot x \quad\Longrightarrow\quad m\ddot x + 2kx = 2kb\sin\omega t$$

The natural frequency is $\omega_n = \sqrt{2k/m}$.

Step 3 · Find the steady-state amplitude

With $x_p = X\sin\omega t$,

$$X = \frac{b}{1 - r^2}, \qquad r = \frac{\omega}{\omega_n}$$

We need $|X| = 2b$, which means $1 - r^2 = \pm\tfrac12$.

Step 4 · Solve for the two frequencies

The plus sign gives the in-phase case below resonance.

$$1 - r^2 = \tfrac12 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r^2 = \tfrac12 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \omega = \omega_n\sqrt{\tfrac12} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}$$

The minus sign gives the out-of-phase case above resonance.

$$1 - r^2 = -\tfrac12 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad r^2 = \tfrac32 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \omega = \omega_n\sqrt{\tfrac32} = \sqrt{\frac{3k}{m}}$$

For the values in the animation, $\omega_n = 10$ rad/s and the two answers are $7.07$ rad/s and $12.25$ rad/s.

Answer

$\omega = \sqrt{k/m}$, where A moves in phase with the box, and $\omega = \sqrt{3k/m}$, where A moves $180°$ out of phase with the box. In both cases $|X| = 2b$.
