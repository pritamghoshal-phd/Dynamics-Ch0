Example 6.B.13A block sticks to a damped carriage

impactdamped free responseinitial conditions

Problem

Block A (mass $2m$) strikes a stationary block B (mass $m$) with a speed of $v$. Upon impact, A sticks to B. Block B is an L-shaped carriage held to a wall by a spring of stiffness $k$ and a dashpot with constant $c$. All surfaces are smooth. The coordinate $x$ is the displacement of B to the left, measured from its rest position, where the spring is unstretched.

Find the time history of motion $x(t)$ of the system after A sticks to B.

Use $v = 30$ m/s, $k = 3000$ N/m, $c = 30$ N·s/m and $m = 10$ kg.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagrams

With x positive to the left, a positive x compresses the spring and the spring pushes the carriage back to the right. The dashpot force opposes the velocity.

During the impact, the only large forces are the equal and opposite contact forces between A and B. The spring and dashpot forces stay finite, so over the very short impact they produce no impulse. After the impact, A and B move together as one body of mass $3m$.

Step 2 · Find the velocity just after the impact

Linear momentum of A and B is conserved during the impact:

$$(2m)\,v + m\,(0) = (3m)\,V_0 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad V_0 = \frac{2}{3}v = \frac{2}{3}(30) = 20\ \text{m/s}$$

The velocity points to the left, so $\dot x(0) = +20$ m/s. The impact is instantaneous, so B has not moved yet and $x(0) = 0$. One third of the kinetic energy of A is lost in the sticking collision.

Step 3 · Write the equation of motion after the impact

For the combined body, Newton’s second law in the $x$ direction (to the left) gives $3m\ddot x = -kx - c\dot x$, so

$$3m\,\ddot x + c\,\dot x + k\,x = 0$$ $$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{3m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3000}{30}} = 10.0\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \zeta = \frac{c}{2\sqrt{3m\,k}} = \frac{30}{2\sqrt{90\,000}} = 0.050$$ $$\omega_d = \omega_n\sqrt{1-\zeta^2} = 9.987\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \zeta\omega_n = 0.50\ \text{s}^{-1}$$
Step 4 · Apply the initial conditions

The underdamped response is $x(t) = e^{-\zeta\omega_n t}(A\cos\omega_d t + B\sin\omega_d t)$. The condition $x(0) = 0$ gives $A = 0$. Then $\dot x(0) = B\,\omega_d = V_0$ gives $B = V_0/\omega_d = 20/9.987 = 2.003$ m.

$$x(t) = 2.003\,e^{-0.50\,t}\sin(9.987\,t)\ \text{m}$$
Answer $$x(t) = \frac{V_0}{\omega_d}e^{-\zeta\omega_n t}\sin\omega_d t = 2.00\,e^{-0.50\,t}\sin(9.99\,t)\ \text{m}, \qquad x \text{ positive to the left}$$

The largest leftward displacement is 1.85 m, reached at $t = 0.152$ s.
