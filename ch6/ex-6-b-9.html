Example 6.B.9Free response of an underdamped system

free responseunderdampedinitial conditions

Problem

A block of mass $m$ is connected to a wall by a spring of stiffness $k$ and a dashpot with damping constant $c$. The block slides on a smooth horizontal surface, and $x$ measures its displacement from equilibrium.

The data are $x_0 = 6$ mm, $c = 32$ kg/s, $k = 1600$ N/m and $m = 1$ kg.

Find the free response $x(t)$ for the initial conditions $x(0) = x_0$ and $\dot x(0) = 0$.

Solution

Every solution starts from free-body diagrams in a displaced position, then writes the equation of motion and reads off the vibration properties. Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Draw the free-body diagram

We draw the block a distance $x$ to the right of equilibrium, moving to the right. The spring pulls it back with $kx$ and the dashpot resists with $c\dot x$. The weight and the normal force are vertical and balance each other.

The floor is smooth, so the vertical forces $N$ and $mg$ balance and only $kx$ and $c\dot x$ act along the motion.

Step 2 · Write the equation of motion and its parameters $$m\ddot x + c\dot x + k x = 0$$ $$\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{1600} = 40\ \text{rad/s}, \qquad \zeta = \frac{c}{2\sqrt{km}} = \frac{32}{2(40)} = 0.400$$

The system is underdamped, so it oscillates at the damped natural frequency

$$\omega_d = \omega_n\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2} = 40\sqrt{0.84} = 36.66\ \text{rad/s}$$
Step 3 · Write the general underdamped response $$x(t) = e^{-\zeta\omega_n t}\left(A\cos\omega_d t + B\sin\omega_d t\right), \qquad \zeta\omega_n = 16\ \text{s}^{-1}$$
Step 4 · Apply the initial conditions

At $t = 0$ the response must equal $x_0$, so $A = x_0 = 6$ mm. Differentiating and setting $\dot x(0) = 0$ gives $-\zeta\omega_n A + \omega_d B = 0$, so

$$B = \frac{\zeta\omega_n\,x_0}{\omega_d} = \frac{16(6)}{36.66} = 2.62\ \text{mm}$$
Step 5 · Write the result $$x(t) = e^{-16t}\left(6\cos 36.66t + 2.62\sin 36.66t\right)\ \text{mm}$$

In amplitude–phase form this is $x(t) = 6.55\,e^{-16t}\cos\left(36.66t - 0.412\right)$ mm, with the amplitude $X = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2} = 6.55$ mm and the phase $\phi = \tan^{-1}(B/A) = 0.412$ rad (23.6°).

Answer

$x(t) = e^{-16t}\left(6\cos 36.66t + 2.62\sin 36.66t\right)$ mm, which is $6.55\,e^{-16t}\cos(36.66t - 0.412)$ mm.
