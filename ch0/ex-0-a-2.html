Example 0.A.2From XY components to rotated xy components

vectorsunit vectorsprojection

Problem

The vector $\mathbf a$ is given in terms of its $XY$ components as

$$\mathbf a = -2\hat{\mathbf I} + 4\hat{\mathbf J}$$

The $xy$ axes are turned counterclockwise from the $XY$ axes by the angle $\theta = 36.87°$, so that $\cos\theta = 0.8$ and $\sin\theta = 0.6$.

Find the $xy$ components of $\mathbf a$.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Sketch the two sets of axes

The grey axes are the fixed $XY$ axes and the purple axes are the $xy$ axes, turned counterclockwise by $\theta$.

Step 2 · Write the new unit vectors in terms of the old ones

Turning the axes counterclockwise by $\theta$ gives

$$\hat{\mathbf i} = \cos\theta\,\hat{\mathbf I} + \sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf J} = 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf I} + 0.6\,\hat{\mathbf J}$$ $$\hat{\mathbf j} = -\sin\theta\,\hat{\mathbf I} + \cos\theta\,\hat{\mathbf J} = -0.6\,\hat{\mathbf I} + 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf J}$$
Step 3 · Project the vector onto each new unit vector

Each component is the dot product of $\mathbf a$ with the corresponding unit vector.

$$a_x = \mathbf a\cdot\hat{\mathbf i} = (-2)(0.8) + (4)(0.6) = 0.8$$ $$a_y = \mathbf a\cdot\hat{\mathbf j} = (-2)(-0.6) + (4)(0.8) = 4.4$$
Step 4 · Check the length

Turning the axes cannot change the length of the vector.

$$|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{(-2)^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{20}, \qquad \sqrt{0.8^2 + 4.4^2} = \sqrt{0.64 + 19.36} = \sqrt{20}$$
Answer

$\mathbf a = 0.8\,\hat{\mathbf i} + 4.4\,\hat{\mathbf j}$
