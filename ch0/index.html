Why a "Chapter 0"?

Dynamics asks one question over and over: given the forces on a body, how does it move? Answering it needs three skills before any new physics appears:

  • Vectors, because force, position, velocity and acceleration all have a direction as well as a size.
  • Free-body diagrams, because Newton's law $\sum \mathbf F = m\mathbf a$ only works if you know exactly which forces act on which body.
  • Calculus of motion, because velocity and acceleration are derivatives of position, and we often know one and need another.

None of this is new mathematics, but a small slip here (a missing force, a sign, a wrong order in a cross product) is the most common reason a dynamics problem goes wrong. Spend time with the animations until the pictures feel automatic.
