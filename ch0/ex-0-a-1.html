Example 0.A.1Vector operations in three dimensions

vectorsdot productcross product

Problem

The vectors $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$ are given in terms of their $xyz$ components as

$$\mathbf a = \hat{\mathbf i} - 3\hat{\mathbf j} + 5\hat{\mathbf k}, \qquad \mathbf b = -2\hat{\mathbf i} - 4\hat{\mathbf j} + 6\hat{\mathbf k}$$

Find (a) $\mathbf a + \mathbf b$, (b) $\mathbf a - \mathbf b$, (c) $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b$, (d) $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b$ and (e) $\mathbf b\times\mathbf a$.

Solution

Try the problem first, and then open one step at a time to check your work.

Step 1 · Sketch the vectors

Before computing anything, it helps to see where the vectors point. Both have a large positive $z$ component and a negative $y$ component, so they point in fairly similar directions. We therefore expect a positive dot product.

Dashed lines trace each vector down to the $xy$ plane. The cross product $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b$ is perpendicular to both $\mathbf a$ and $\mathbf b$, and $\mathbf b\times\mathbf a$ points the opposite way.

Step 2 · Add and subtract component by component

Vectors are added and subtracted by adding and subtracting their like components.

$$\mathbf a + \mathbf b = (1-2)\hat{\mathbf i} + (-3-4)\hat{\mathbf j} + (5+6)\hat{\mathbf k} = -\hat{\mathbf i} - 7\hat{\mathbf j} + 11\hat{\mathbf k}$$ $$\mathbf a - \mathbf b = (1+2)\hat{\mathbf i} + (-3+4)\hat{\mathbf j} + (5-6)\hat{\mathbf k} = 3\hat{\mathbf i} + \hat{\mathbf j} - \hat{\mathbf k}$$
Step 3 · Compute the dot product

The dot product is the sum of the products of like components.

$$\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b = (1)(-2) + (-3)(-4) + (5)(6) = -2 + 12 + 30 = 40$$

As a check, $|\mathbf a| = \sqrt{35}$ and $|\mathbf b| = \sqrt{56}$, so $\cos\theta = 40/\sqrt{35\cdot 56} = 0.904$ and the angle between the vectors is $\theta = 25.4°$. The vectors are indeed close in direction.

Step 4 · Compute the cross product a × b

The cross product can be written as a determinant whose first row holds the unit vectors.

$$\mathbf a\times\mathbf b = \begin{vmatrix}\hat{\mathbf i}&\hat{\mathbf j}&\hat{\mathbf k}\\ 1&-3&5\\ -2&-4&6\end{vmatrix} = \big[(-3)(6)-(5)(-4)\big]\hat{\mathbf i} - \big[(1)(6)-(5)(-2)\big]\hat{\mathbf j} + \big[(1)(-4)-(-3)(-2)\big]\hat{\mathbf k}$$
$$\mathbf a\times\mathbf b = 2\hat{\mathbf i} - 16\hat{\mathbf j} - 10\hat{\mathbf k}$$

As a check, $(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf a = 2 + 48 - 50 = 0$ and $(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b)\cdot\mathbf b = -4 + 64 - 60 = 0$. The result is perpendicular to both vectors, as it must be.

Step 5 · Reverse the order for b × a

Reversing the order of a cross product changes its sign, so no new computation is needed.

$$\mathbf b\times\mathbf a = -\,\mathbf a\times\mathbf b = -2\hat{\mathbf i} + 16\hat{\mathbf j} + 10\hat{\mathbf k}$$
Answer

(a) $\mathbf a + \mathbf b = -\hat{\mathbf i} - 7\hat{\mathbf j} + 11\hat{\mathbf k}$

(b) $\mathbf a - \mathbf b = 3\hat{\mathbf i} + \hat{\mathbf j} - \hat{\mathbf k}$

(c) $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b = 40$

(d) $\mathbf a\times\mathbf b = 2\hat{\mathbf i} - 16\hat{\mathbf j} - 10\hat{\mathbf k}$

(e) $\mathbf b\times\mathbf a = -2\hat{\mathbf i} + 16\hat{\mathbf j} + 10\hat{\mathbf k}$
